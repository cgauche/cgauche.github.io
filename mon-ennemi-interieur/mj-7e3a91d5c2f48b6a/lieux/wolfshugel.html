Wolfshügel MJ

Wolfshügel (Mother's Mound)

Sacred hill maintenu par Initiates des Old Faith (Taal+Ulric+Rhya+Manann). Mother's Mound originel où Sigmar fit bénir Ghal Maraz par Ulric (canon ch.12 l.149-160 via Albertus Nyman). Sacred Flame of Ulric recharge runes Ghal Maraz drained. Scène bataille horde Drexol the Extinction ch.12 climax.

Identité

  • Type : Hill sacré + petit temple at heart hill. Ancient pre-Sigmar.
  • Localisation : ~1 jour Diesdorf (via ancient pathway moss-covered ogham stones).
  • Visible from any high vantage point miles around — stands above forest.
  • Tags : #Wolfshügel #MothersMound #Taal #Ulric #Rhya #Manann #SacredFlame #OldFaith #GhalMaraz #Recharge #Drexol #Krukan #Albenriek #Arc7 #Ch12

Source

  • EiR ch.12 l.146-160 Albertus Nyman fragments parchment : Mother's Mound = where Sigmar stopped + bénir Ghal Maraz by Ulric.
  • EiR ch.12 l.231-339 Wolfshügel + bataille Drexol horde.
  • EiR ch.12 l.560-576 The Temple + The Renewal canon Sacred Flame Hammer recharge.

Histoire

  • Sacred long before Sigmar was born (canon ch.12 l.279). « First fire of Ulric was lit » possibly.
  • Built by followers Taal+Rhya+Ulric Elder days for gods watch over their domain (canon ch.12 l.279).
  • Pilgrimage Empire-wide centuries — declined when Ulric worship focused North + newer gods.
  • Still maintained by initiates older gods — view site as holy.
  • Sigmar passed through during final journey : prostration devant Ulric + Hammer placed in Sacred Flame Ulric + renewed = recharge ritual.

Géographie

Le Wolfsruhe (Wolf's Rest) — niveau bas

  • 8 stone domed huts around base hill.
  • 2 huts dismantled : stones used fill access tunnel temple.
  • Brother Adric hut : caretaker.
  • 4 huts home recently arrived refugees Albenriek (32 personnes : 21 adultes + 11 enfants).
  • 2 wagons + 3 ponies tethered (4th hunting).

Le hill central — sacred ground

  • Steep packed earthen slope leads upper level. Comfortable wide enough cart.
  • 6-ft sturdy stone wall runs around outside upper level. Recently fallen down 2 points (stones at base ou bottom slope).
  • Forest grown towards base — trees overhang upper level. Lighter person climb trees + drop over wall.

Le temple — passageway sealed

  • Short flight worn, moss-covered stone steps au bottom of hill.
  • Small tunnel entrée filled with rocks. Solidly blocked : clearing requires extended Challenging +0 Strength Test 35 SL (1 hour labour per test, Hard -20 Endurance or Fatigued).
  • Restricted space : one person at a time.

Le temple interior canon ch.12 l.560

  • Passageway 15-ft long narrow tunnel. Dwarf height initial → Human crawl final feet stomach.
  • Symbols Ulric + Taal + Rhya + Manann carved walls.
  • Main chamber : musty + dusty. Peaceful + safe feeling. Walls rough hacked.
  • Centre : low stone enclosure filled with smooth black rocks. Centre : ancient bronze statue howling wolf size large fist.
  • Sacred Flame mechanism : « small dial attached to a disc lies inside the mouth of the wolf statue. Turning the dial causes the discs to turn and disappear downwards, with flints causing sparks that light an odourless gas that pours from the Wolfhead and settles on the stones burning with a heatless silver flame ».
  • Light flames = casting strange moving shadows Wolfs+Bears+Elks roaming walls.
  • 3 smaller chambers : 2 holes Old Gods small statues (removed) + 1 empty chamber priests (polished red stones).

Ghal Maraz Renewal ch.12 l.569-576

  • Place Hammer in Sacred Flame → starts glow.
  • Runes burst blazing white light one by one — impossibly bright versions cast walls, each clearly seen.
  • Tarnish burns away → Hammer luminous gold rippling internal fire.
  • Soundless wave pulse outwards — all present feel.
  • Hammer feels light when picked up.
  • Fires runes slowly fade but still glitter when hefted/swung.
  • Hammer's power renewed. Awakened.

Sacred Flame Wolfshügel = recharge Ghal Maraz dormant runes

  • Rune of Fire (was inactive Caves of Chaos) → reactive.
  • Rune of Stalwart → reactive.
  • Rune of Spell-Breaking → reactive.

Bataille Drexol horde ch.12 l.342-581

Timeline Day 3 :

  • Mid-morning Hans returns news horde.
  • Early evening Thirteen arrives.
  • Day 3 early morning : horde arrives.
  • Day 3 mid-morning : bataille.

Composition canon

  • Defenders max : PJ + Brother Adric + Sergeant Major Oldheim + 8 soldats + 21 adult refugees + Thirteen.
  • Attaquants : Drexol the Extinction + Krukan + 8 Gors + 22 Ungors + 30 Mutants.

Phases canon

  1. Initial scare 5 turns Drexol + Gors maintain Mutants screaming line.
  2. 2 smaller groups perimeter (2 Gors + 11 Ungors each) seek wall weaknesses.
  3. Charge frontal ramp Drexol + main force.
  4. Krukan climbs wall non-combattants ou attack defenders behind.

Pertes canon

  • Si entrée barricaded : every 5 rounds 1 soldat + 3 refugees + 1 Gor/2 Ungors + 5 mutants OUT.
  • Barricade falls < 15 defenders. Fighting Wolfsruhe.
  • Every 5 rounds : 2 soldats + 5 refugees + 1 Gor/2 Ungors + 3 mutants OUT.

Si Ghal Maraz renouvelé + utilisé bataille

  • Horde turn within rounds :
    • Mutants flee immédiate.
    • Drexol stands fight. Si tombe = horde routs.
