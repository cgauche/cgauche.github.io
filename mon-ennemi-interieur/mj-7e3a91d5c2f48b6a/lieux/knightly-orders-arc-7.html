# Knightly Orders Arc 7

> Fiche groupée des **Ordres de Chevaliers** rencontrés Arc 7. Pour Chevaliers du Loup Blanc voir fiche Chevaliers du Loup Blanc (Arc 5+). Pour Reiksguard voir Reiksguard dédiée. Cette fiche couvre **Chevaliers Panthères + Knights of the Fiery Heart + Knights Griffon + Knights of the Blazing Sun + Order of the Silver Hammer**.

## Chevaliers Panthères (Middenheim chapter + Altdorf chapter)

> **Order militaire séculier** sworn défendre **Imperial Union integrity**. Considérés *« archetypal knight »* canon Intro l.509. **Middenheim chapter** = strangement loyal Todbringer (canon Intro l.509) — *« Implacable + Supportive Talents fighting in defence Todbringers »*.

### Source canon
- **EiR Intro l.508-511** : *« Dedicated to the integrity of Imperial Union, the Knights Panther are considered the archetypal knight. Those in the service of Middenheim are strangely dedicated to the Graf and his family »*.

### Personnages canon
- **Veiran Thugenheim** First Knight Chevaliers Panthères Middenheim. Wields **The Fang of Horros** sword.
- **Preceptor Leberecht von Hardenburg** : *« much decorated Knight Panther »* Castle Reikguard. Annonce Bretonnia alliance Blood Keep canon ch.4 l.146.
- **Chevaliers Panthères Altdorf chapter** : 6 knights vanguard Altdorf delegation Shining Rock + 12 knights rearguard Middenheim delegation.

### Rôle Arc 7
- **S57 vanguard délégation Altdorf** Middenheim.
- **S58 Shining Rock** : 6 Altdorf + 12 Middenheim.
- **S59 Castle Reikguard** : escorte PJ Reiksguard.
- **Climax ch.13** : *« march forth in force ensure mobs do not trouble capital's more affluent areas »* (canon ch.8 l.10).
- Tags : `#KnightsPanther` `#Veiran` `#Hardenburg` `#Middenheim` `#Altdorf` `#ImperialUnion`

## Knights of the Fiery Heart

> **Sigmarite Templar Order** require former Priest of Sigmar. **Miracles Sigmar Trait** + specific miracles : **Beacon Righteous Virtue, Sigmar's Fiery Hammer, Soulfire**.

### Source canon
- **EiR Intro l.513-514** : *« In order to join the order of the Fiery Heart a Knight must have spent some time as a Priest of Sigmar »*.

### Personnages canon
- **Cleric-Captain Konrad Metternich** : commander Sigmarite knights défense Shining Rock attack canon ch.3 l.224. Orders : *« All righteous men of Sigmar to me! We will not be mocked! »*.

### Rôle Arc 7
- **S58 Shining Rock** : équal numbers Knights Griffon + Knights of the Fiery Heart + Altdorf chapter Chevaliers Panthères guard Yorri 20+ total.
- **Climax ch.13** : Volkshalle defense possible.
- Tags : `#KnightsFieryHeart` `#Sigmar` `#Sigmarite` `#Templar` `#Konrad Metternich`

## Knights Griffon

> **Sigmarite Order** — bodyguard senior priest Sigmar. *« 2 Knights Griffon »* escort Grand Theogonist Yorri canon ch.5 l.83.

### Source canon
- **EiR ch.5 l.83** S60 défilé : *« accompagné de deux Knights Griffon, le Grand Theogonist est habillé en full priestly regalia »*.
- **EiR ch.5 timeline défilé Knights Griffon** parade : Grandmaster Bacham takes umbrage at their place in parade.

### Rôle Arc 7
- **S58 Shining Rock** Yorri escort.
- **S60 défilé Altdorf** Yorri pavilion.
- **Climax ch.13** Volkshalle Yorri presence.
- **Grandmaster Bacham** mentionné canon ch.5 défilé final regiments.
- Tags : `#KnightsGriffon` `#Sigmar` `#Yorri` `#Bacham` `#Bodyguard`

## Order of the Silver Hammer (Witch Hunters)

> **Preeminent Sigmarite Witch Hunter institution**. **Krieglitz chasse cultistes** post-Turmoil canon. **Quintus Fassbinder a several contacts** with them.

### Source canon
- **EiR ch.8 l.145** : *« He has also proved invaluable in the discovery of several cultists and witches and he has several contacts in the Order of the Silver Hammer, the preeminent Sigmarite institution of Witch Hunters »*.
- **EiR History of Turmoil l.34** : *« Once the Order of the Silver Hammer had finished with the Krieglitzes »* — post-Turmoil chasse Krieglitz.

### Rôle Arc 7
- **Post-Turmoil canon** : chasse Krieglitz Talabecland + Talabheim. Cultistes purge.
- **Fassbinder contact** : peut faciliter Witch Hunter aid si PJ besoin Altdorf.
- Tags : `#OrderSilverHammer` `#WitchHunters` `#Sigmar` `#Krieglitz` `#Fassbinder`
