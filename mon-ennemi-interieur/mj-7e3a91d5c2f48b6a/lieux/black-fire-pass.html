Black Fire Pass MJ

Black Fire Pass

One of most historically + politically + spiritually important landmarks Old World. Where Sigmar united tribes vs Greenskins 2500 years ago. Major trade route Empire south + invasion risk Greenskins. Sites majeurs Arc 7 ch.10-11 : Bloodmoss Plain + Black Fire Keep + Stations of Exodus + Vithang Dum + Three Mothers + Zan Mingol + Schmerzeit Mine + Lost Gorge + Caves of Chaos.

Identité

  • Type : Mountain pass Black Mountains entre Empire south + Border Princes.
  • Histoire canon : Sigmar united tribes defying Greenskins, sécurisant lands north. Brigundians oath garde mouth pass post-victoire. Averland ruler = « Protector of Black Fire » title.
  • Old Dwarf Road runs bottom pass. Maintained Imperial Dwarfs Karaz Ankor.
  • Dwarf history : Haz Drazh Kadrin = nom dwarf. War of Vengeance battles vs Asur.
  • Greenskin invasions : Gorbad Ironclaw 1709 IC + Grom the Paunch (700+ ans plus tard) + future canon Third Battle Black Fire Pass.

Géographie canon

Pass

  • Sheer sides slightly glassy patina réfléchit sun bas.
  • Old Dwarf Road : paved surface even, free plant growth (Imperial Dwarfs maintenance).
  • Way forts + watch towers scattered — many disrepair, occupied intermittently par Averland Mountainguard + Bergjaeger (rangers scouts).
  • Notable watchtower : Dok a Dum (canon ch.10 l.111) Dwarf tower volcanic rock projects cliff face striking angle. Sole remaining Dwarf 'Sentinels'. Garrisoned Averland 8th Halberds.

Bloodmoss Plain (canon ch.10 l.51)

  • Vallée mouth Black Fire Pass. Reddish moss sur rocky outcrops.
  • Sigmar's forces mustered before famous battle.
  • The Place of the Muster : sturdy stone building alongside Old Dwarf Road. Shrine Sigmar painted names tribal chieftains who pledged. Pilgrims often.

Black Fire Keep (canon ch.10 l.66)

  • 5 miles into pass. Mountainous walls < 1 mile apart.
  • Raised granite hill. Ugly towers vantage east+west.
  • Massive walls blackened many Greenskin sieges.
  • Ground cleared vegetation+rocks = killing space.
  • Banners Karl-Franz + Averland fly battlements.
  • Permanent garrison Averland State Troops Mountainguard — hardiest soldiers, well-equipped, arrogant.
  • « First real line of defence from invasion into the Empire ».
  • « Mountainguard tend to see all those in 'rearguard' behind them as soft ». Very unlikely party admitted to Keep unless dispensation Averland elite.

Stations of the Exodus (canon ch.10 l.103)

  • 5 shrines trace Sigmar's final journey :
    1. Shrine of the Dawn.
    2. Shrine of the Boar.
    3. Shrine of the Wolf.
    4. Shrine of the Dusk.
    5. Shrine of the Divine Comet (most sacred, close Vithang Dum).
  • Most : carved stone columns ou decorated wooden frames. Comets + Sigmar with boar + wolf companions.

The Battleground + Temple of the Holy Founding (canon ch.10 l.119)

  • Narrow point pass where Sigmar defeated Bloodfang's Greenskin horde + united Empire.
  • Row huge boulders Sigmar positioned army, stand across valley.
  • Lengthy prayers Sigmar engraved devotional spirals sur boulders.
  • The Eagle's Nest : 30-foot rock from which Sigmar launched himself into Orc hordes swinging Ghal Maraz.
  • Temple of the Holy Founding : 9th or 10th temple here (location vulnerable attack). Father Otto Rotmund — « cannot speak after he lost his tongue when tortured by Beastmen in Drakwald ». 5-10 pilgrims penance.

Vithang Dum (canon ch.10 l.125)

  • Dwarf way station orig. trade caravans.
  • Still in use : travellers shelter horses + austere common room + basic victuals.
  • Urd Flintbane warden (dour).
  • Low ceilings + soft furnishings short but fortified.
  • Dog Peak Pass meets Black Fire just beyond Battleground at Vithang Dum.

The Three Mothers (canon ch.10 l.149)

  • ~1 mile from Vithang Dum.
  • Group Dwarf ancestor statues : Valaya + 2 other Dwarf women.
  • Finely carved cliff face. Intact despite age.
  • Passing Dwarfs pay respect = representative all Dwarf mothers.
  • Opposite entrance gorge concealed in rockfall = entrée Lost Gorge canon.

Zan Mingol (canon ch.10 l.152)

  • 2 miles south Three Mothers.
  • Ruined Dwarf watch tower : little more sturdy foundation reddish granite.
  • Khazalid runes by old gatehouse identify name lost bastion.

Lost Gorge canon ch.10 l.162-269

  • 35 miles long. Madcraw Clan Night Goblins territory (shaman Cacklespit).
  • Sides steep + strewn loose boulders + scree. Sparse vegetation.
  • Stream trickles down to fill pool at rockfall.
  • 3 sections :
    • Lower Gorge 20 miles : open. Scouts only.
    • Pinch Point : cliffs almost touch. Cacklespit paintings.
    • Deep Ravine 5 miles : permanent semi-darkness. Madcraw Tunnels canon system.
    • Northern Gorge 10 miles : wider valley, Cacklespit avoids (Sheerargetru influence). Abandoned camp + Purple Cap colonies + Waterfalls.

Schmerzeit Mine (canon ch.10 l.169 + ch.8 l.279)

  • Teugen, Steinhäger, Haagen Mining Concern — permit signed 9 Brauzeit 2508 IC at Averburg Palace, 50% partenaires + 25% Averheim treasury + 25% King Alrik Ranulfsson Karak Hirn.
  • Tunnel dug through rockfall by Gideon manipulation.
  • Miners camp : 2 tents + corpses Lenz + Knoch (canon ch.10 l.173). Dieter miner survived early, joined Faithful Forty pilgrims.

Caves of Chaos (canon ch.11)

  • Behind waterfall at end Lost Gorge.
  • 9 cavernes thématiques Vents de la magie (Ghur+Ulgu+Chamon+Azyr+Shyish+Aqshy+Hysh+Ghyran+Dhar).
  • Sheerargetru sealed canon ch.11.
  • Ghal Maraz resting place (canon ch.11 l.451).

South canon ch.10 l.155

  • Vossheim merchant camp + Kazad Kolzharr Dwarf Keep + Munzig Border Princes town.
  • « If Characters go this far, they have overshot their destination and Gideon appears disguised as itinerant priest to set them back on their path ».

Inns canon ch.10 l.30

3 well-fortified inns + villages between Grenzstadt + Black Fire Keep : Griffon's Head, Stalwart Knave, Armoured Turnip — « renowned for good service, stout walls, and tough customers ».
