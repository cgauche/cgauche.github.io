Arc 7 ch.9 — Voyage Altdorf → Col du Feu Noir

Schertal + Graubach + Florianfeld (canon ch.9 l.117-167)

  • Villages razed par Wissenland soldats Baron Klaus von Bachmann.
  • Old Faith predator-prey ancien — Sigmar iconographie subvertie.
  • Schertal temple : monolith ancient + half-natural half-carved predator-prey symbol embarqué refugees cargo boat.
  • Reikland-Wissenland border sur Reik river.

Schloss Bachmann (canon ch.9 l.176-201)

  • Castle Wissenland sur ridge 20 miles Schertal. Commanding views Reik river stretch.
  • Baron Klaus von Bachmann + 100+ men-at-arms. Approach diplomatique ou attack foolhardy.

Nuln docks (canon ch.9 l.202-280)

  • Carroburg arrive harbourmaster's office → Hans Knees spy bureaucratic delays Emmanuelle.
  • Green Waterwheel inn : recommended Knees, Winona barmaid spy.
  • Sven dockhand : autre spy ground près Carroburg.
  • 2 jours dismantle rigging + traffic press jam.
  • Aldig Quarter : Emmanuelle's fortified mansion alternate location si ch.12 retour (Volkshalle trop watched).

Heidheinheim (canon ch.9 l.286-301)

  • Village Stirland bank Aver, between Nuln + Streissen.
  • Threshers Fione Brecht raid : Sudenland soldats disguised Nuln false flag.
  • Threshers raiders 8 + 3 layers uniforms (Nuln / Goats / Sudenland buff).

Streissen (canon ch.9 l.303-336 + ch.12 l.85-122)

  • Second city Averland. Freistadt charter 2456 IC progressive university.
  • Streissen College : small influential university + prestigious medical school.
  • Independence Party (SIP) coup sous Marcus Karlssen — false revolution Leitdorf op.
  • Alptraum's Winter Residence : Leitdorf post-coup residence + purges.
  • Deep Haus gaol : secret executions purges.
  • 2502 IC bread riots mémoire collective.

Averheim (canon ch.9 l.442-447 + ch.12 l.69)

  • Capital Averland, sur bluff above Aver. Averburg citadel Elector Counts depuis Siggurd Companion Sigmar.
  • Western Clock Tower : Grimshank's Chain entry point (Khardrif Inglepeak Boatman Pegasus barge).
  • Hostile post-Leitdorf intervention si PJ mediated bad : ejected ou welcomed.

Grenzstadt (canon ch.9 l.449-501)

  • Last major settlement before Old Dwarf Road rise mountains.
  • Last place Sigmar stayed before leaving Empire = major Sigmar pilgrimage.
  • Temple Sigmar vast + wealthy. Capitular Almeric Zischler high priest.
  • Eternal Watch tower : tallest building. Part temple + lookout + campanile. Vigil Sigmar return Col du Feu Noir.
  • Night Vigil hidden cult : kills tomb robbers. Rauf Wasserburg best agent.
  • Three-Axe Brewery : Grimshank's Chain Dwarf entry point.

Arc 7 ch.12 — Retour Col du Feu Noir → Altdorf

Lengenfeld (canon ch.12 l.178)

  • Small town west Streissen. Market Gate dusk : Grimshank's Chain meeting.
  • Durkan Orkisson (Dwarf blacksmith specialising agricultural tools).
  • Iron Wind sailing ship Captain Fimbur Dimzadson : Nuln → Kemperbad transit.

Diesdorf (canon ch.12 l.232-244)

  • Town near Wolfshügel (1 day journey).
  • Reikland Halberds barracks près (Sergeant Major Oldheim).
  • Ancient pathway depuis river vers Wolfshügel (moss-covered ogham stones).

Wolfshügel approche (canon ch.12 l.234-240)

  • Forest journey half-day.
  • Beastman scouts ambush Drexol's : Fox-Head Beastman scout + 2 Beastmen vs soldats Reikland Oldheim.

Albenriek (canon ch.12 l.320)

  • Village Barren Hills near Torpin. Razed par strange beasts (Middenlanders ou Ostlanders accusés).
  • 32 refugees (21 adultes + 11 enfants) settled Wolfsruhe 5 huts.

Kemperbad (existing fiche Kemperbad enrichir)

  • Cult of the Broken Fingers : Martin Schmidt + 4 cultistes splinter Main Pourpre.
  • Able Kazriksson + nephew Gurrin Ablesniz workshop (cellar refuge PJ ch.12 l.207-228).
  • Ablesniz throat slit + workshop arson : Cult Broken Fingers attack.

Arc 7 ch.13 climax — Altdorf

Imperial Palace bedchamber Karl-Franz (canon ch.8 l.105+)

  • Karl-Franz alité large 4-poster bed silk drapes + griffon bedposts.
  • Henrik Kappelmuller spends almost all time in room + small antechamber sleeps.
  • Pomander Schlafenkraut + Moonflower + pine sous lit : Hard -20 Perception. Very Hard -30 Willpower test or Fatigued (4+ turns).

Grand Cathedral of Sigmar Altdorf (canon ch.6)

  • Wedding venue S61 : Katarina + Wolfgang mariage interrompu par Barthelm.
  • Side doors : Wasmeier Pink Fire entrée latérale.
  • Domplatz cemetery alleys : Wasmeier chase post-S61.
  • Grande Bibliothèque Sigmar : annexe — coffre tomes interdits + traités hérétiques. Knights of the Fiery Heart guards + senior priests Order of Anvil vetting. « only the wisest + most trusted priests Sigmar can enter » (canon Source/Altdorf - Crown of the Empire/06 - CHAPTER 5 - THE SOUTH BANK.md:1121-1126).

Volkshalle (canon ch.7 + ch.13)

  • Imperial conference hall + ceremony venue. Octagonal great chamber.
  • Throne stone : Emperor stage.
  • Reliquary new gold+silver (Albertus Goldformer) : diamond button trapdoor → 30ft drop → wine cellar.
  • Wine cellar canon : built 1149 IC Emperor Ludwig the Fat elevator throne (removed long ago, Gideon uncovered).
  • Gallery octagonal + Refectory : entourages observers gallery during conclave.
  • Reiksguard guarded entrances + mezzanine.

Manoir Helstein (Mauerblumchen, Altdorf)

  • « For political reasons the Todbringers do not keep their own estate in Altdorf, but stay at the lavish townhouse of their loyal subjects, the noble Helsteins of Middenheim ».
  • PJ residence Arc 7 S60-S62 + assault S63 homebrew Boris mort.

Volkshalle Wine Cellar (canon ch.13 l.285)

  • Built 1149 IC Ludwig the Fat : trône-elevator descend cellar pour boire post-meetings. Trône relocated + elevator removed + hole covered long ago.
  • Heavy wooden door + stout lock. Hermine only key. Hard -20 Pick Lock.
  • Rare vintage wines + brandy casks. Old bottles dust + cobwebs.
  • Hole ceiling under Reliquary : antique mais récemment uncovered Gideon's artisans.
