Grimshank's Chain MJ

Grimshank's Chain

Ancient Dwarf network activé pour transmettre messages + goods importants long distance. Rarement activé. Members usually eldest + most respected of community. Knowledge passed within families oral tradition. Token = Grimshank's coin (rune-marked). PJ reçoivent ch.12 d'Iron Marshall Kina Hammerwatch post-récupération Ghal Maraz.

Identité

  • Type : Informal Dwarf network transport+protection messengers important messages/goods.
  • Motto ch.12 l.38 : « Our ancestors, our steel, our battle line — only broken at the weakest part ».
  • Activation : informal, activé members en cas besoin. Rare.
  • Function : transmission important messages + goods over long distances. Support + protect messenger.
  • Tags : #GrimshanksChain #Chain #Dwarf #Transport #Network #Grimshank #Coin #Arc7 #Ch12 #Hammerwatch

Source

  • EiR ch.12 l.37-46 Grimshank's Chain description complète.
  • EiR ch.12 l.6-32 The First Link — Hammerwatch activate Chain pour PJ.

Structure

  • Members usually among eldest + most respected of community.
  • Others may be asked / recruited undertake aims without knowledge Chain.
  • Knowledge passed within families oral tradition.
  • Sites linked Chain locations : public site checked daily at dusk by member.

Activation

  • Any member at any time.
  • Great seriousness + trust.
  • Members undertake responsibilities upon presented with Grimshank's coin : « token marked with rune of Grimshank, handed to those travelling along Chain with clear instructions of where it should be taken ».
  • Plus coin expensive + plus message important.
  • Runes painted coin indicate when coin was issued = use specific + limited.

Chain members ch.12 PJ trajet

Iron Marshall Kina Hammerwatch (canon ch.12 l.7)

  • First Link — Col du Feu Noir post-Hammer recovery.
  • Donne premier coin + directs Three-Axe Brewery Grenzstadt.

Gararoth (canon ch.12 l.68)

  • Three-Axe Brewery Grenzstadt. « particularly taciturn Dwarf ».
  • Transport via large covered wagon with false half-barrels Grenzstadt → Averheim.
  • Hands new gold crown different blue runes : « You should go to the Western Clock Tower after dark if you intend to keep following The Chain ».

Khardrif Inglepeak (canon ch.12 l.73)

  • Dwarf Boatman Western Clock Tower Averheim.
  • Barge The Pegasus moored next The Hidden Valley inn.
  • « Hides them in a compartment below deck that smells faintly of Weirdroot ». Averheim → Streissen.

Durkan Orkisson (canon ch.12 l.178)

  • Lengenfeld Market Gate dusk.
  • Blacksmith specialising agricultural tools. « Old even for a Dwarf and moves slowly with the aid of two engraved sticks ». Business now run by grandson same name.
  • Si Hammer présenté : « tears well in his eyes. 'By the Ancestors, lads, the tales are true. Five runes. But it's sleeping, I think. Its fire must be rekindled.' »

Captain Fimbur Dimzadson (canon ch.12 l.180)

  • The Iron Wind sailing ship Lengenfeld area.
  • Old sailor + tall tales travel + adventure.
  • Smuggle PJ past Nuln : « his good name, and being a Dwarf, gets him and his crew through ». Cargo Averlander wine inspected.
  • Nuln → Kemperbad.

Able Kazriksson + nephew Gurrin Ablesniz (canon ch.12 l.211)

  • Kemperbad workshop.
  • Kazriksson member Chain « never seen it activated before ». Excited honour serve.
  • Sends Ablesniz get fresh food.
  • Ablesniz kills par Cult of the Broken Fingers night attack (canon ch.12 l.221) : throat slit + chest of tools dragged onto trapdoor.
