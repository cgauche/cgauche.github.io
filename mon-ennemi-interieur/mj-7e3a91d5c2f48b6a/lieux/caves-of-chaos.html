Caves of Chaos MJ

Caves of Chaos

Caverne profonde Black Mountains où Sigmar piégea Sheerargetru 2500 ans + abandonna Ghal Maraz comme sceau magique. Accès derrière waterfall end Lost Gorge. 9 caverns thématiques Vents de la magie + Resting Place Ghal Maraz centre. Chaque caverne = corruption + magic suffused + monster guardian.

Identité

  • Type : Cave system Black Mountains — Sheerargetru's prison + Hammer storage.
  • Localisation : behind waterfall at end Lost Gorge, Col du Feu Noir.
  • Status canon : « Within these caves, the adventurers are Tzeentch's playthings. He toys with them for amusement ». Gideon wants PJ succeed retrieve Hammer.
  • Tags : #CavesOfChaos #Sheerargetru #GhalMaraz #Tzeentch #9Winds #9Caverns #BlackFirePass #LostGorge #ChaosHound #Ghosts #TreacherousHerald #ChaosMinotaur #Harpies #PinkBlueHorrors #Adelfried #Arc7 #Ch11

Source

  • EiR ch.11 entire chapter Caves of Chaos (canon l.1-543).

Entrée (ch.11 l.13-19)

  • Pass through waterfall → gaping cavern massive, walls+ceiling vanish darkness.
  • Cathedral-like stalagmite spires + colossal stalactites dripping icy water.
  • Stalagmites visibly growing barely perceptibly = time unnaturally quickened.
  • Si turn around : waterfall vanished, replaced by impenetrable blackness. Only forward.
  • Tunnel exits eastwards : twists+turns + jagged blades rock.

Les 9 cavernes ch.11

Location 1 — Lair Great Beast (Ghur — Lore of Beasts +1 SL)

  • Wet animal fur stench. Vile slush + bones+skulls.
  • Three-Headed Chaos Hound : massive hound shimmering turquoise fur, 3 wolfish heads, scorpion tail.
  • Elthrai Cadaith Elven sword treasure : ancient blade, magical, Fast+Impale+Precise qualities.

Location 2 — Shades of Despair (Ulgu — Lore Shadows +1 SL)

  • Mist tendrils. Voices echo mocking. Rattled Condition (new : +10 Perception / -10 other).
  • Ghosts (canon WFRP p.331) entrapped spirits previous adventuring party Emperor Ludwig I era ~1500 ans. Soothsaying 6 clues (Wolfgang vessel/Kappelmuller/Sheerargetru etc.).

Location 3 — Temptation (Chamon — Lore Metal +1 SL)

  • Molten gold lake burns/melts. Path island center. Rainbow aurora.
  • Treacherous Herald Daemon Tzeentch — blind, named character, 4 gifts corrupt : feather (Tzeentch's Firestorm), eye (Bolt of Change), claw (Mindfire), tongue (Pink Horror killer + creates 2 Blue).
  • Treasures gold/silver = curse : devient lead quitter location.

Location 4 — Tempest (Azyr — Lore Heavens +1 SL)

  • Howling wind. Smooth rocks moist/warm = flesh.
  • Swarms Rainbow Snakes : kaleidoscope hypnotic. Venom → Fatigued + minor Corruption.

Location 5 — Furious Dead (Shyish — Lore Death +1 SL)

  • Portal grinning skull. Underground river of blood. Jabberslythe skeleton bones useable raft.
  • Zombies (Hatred Characters) — waves attack crossing.

Location 6 — Fire and Horror (Aqshy — Lore Fire +1 SL)

  • Maze fiery pits multicoloured flames. Choking sulphuric fumes.
  • Pink Horrors + Blue Horrors : leap fire pits. Split : Pink kills → 2 Blues. Tzeentch's Fire : pool spells. Pink Fire / Blue Fire of Tzeentch.

Location 7 — False Prophecy (Hysh — Lore Light +1 SL)

  • Blinding white light maze. Blinded Condition all Characters.
  • Chaos Minotaur Lord : bull-headed 2x height man. Bronze breastplate Tzeentch sigil horns. Golden daemonic mask.
  • The Prophet : Adelfried Jarmund trapped crystal shard 1700 IC. « Heretic Verena pledged Tzeentch life ». Sows suspicion PJ post-rescue.

Location 8 — Hellwood (Ghyran — Lore Life +1 SL)

  • Luminous moss + ferns. Leafless trees twisted human forms.
  • Harpies (3 per Character) : grapple+torture victims. Steal items flee treetops.

Location 9 — The Prison (Dhar — Dark Magic +1 SL)

  • Inky slime + sticky tar drops from darkness. Bitter taste sweet smell.
  • Glimmer aurora : massive black boulder + Ghal Maraz embedded. Tar leaches up.
  • The Resting Place of Ghal Maraz — moderate Corruption.
  • Treachery Unbound canon : Hammer pickup → torrent magical energy → daemons unstable destroyed + magical environments stripped + Character corruption→wounds permanent loss.

Ghal Maraz (ch.11 l.460-491)

  • 2-Handed Warhammer SB+7 Damaging Fine 3 Pummel Unbreakable.
  • Radiant Nimbus : Unstable Creatures lose 3 Wounds/turn.
  • Smednir's Master Rune of Head-Wrecking : +40 head crits.
  • Rune of Goblin Bane : Impact vs Orc/Goblin/Snotling/Hobgoblin. Fear 1 vs Goblins.
  • *Rune of Fire (inactive — needs Sacred Flame Wolfshügel) : Ablaze.
  • *Rune of Stalwart (inactive) : +2 SL Cool vs Fear+Terror.
  • *Rune of Spell-Breaking (inactive) : -2 SL hostile spells casting.

Helping Hands (ch.11 l.40-61)

3 events possible si PJ struggling :

  • Boon of Sigmar : footprints + golden light + remove Corruption.
  • Sacred Relic Marius Luxus : skeleton + warhammer broken. Restore Fate or Resilience.
  • Gift of Shallya : white shape + crevice bottle. Healing 1d10 W per dose (4 doses).

Storm exit (ch.11 l.493-503)

  • Exit caves = almighty storm at night awaits. Waterfalls crash + stream torrent.
  • Mine flooded : broad tunnel 2 feet water+mud emerge Col du Feu Noir.
  • Madcraw Clan : Goblins drowned storm. Survivors avoid PJ avec Hammer (canon ch.11 l.482-485) → Goblins feel power Hammer Greenskin hatred. Cacklespit foresaw.
