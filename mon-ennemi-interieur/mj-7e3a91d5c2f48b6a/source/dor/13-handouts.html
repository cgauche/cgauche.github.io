<a class="line-anchor" id="L26"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L27"></a>The Heavens were filled with a Greate Number of Shootinge Stars and some of these did seem to fall upon the Ground which groaned under their assault. <a class="line-anchor" id="L28"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a>Being Forewarned by the earlier events of Hexenstag, I was able to trace the course of a particularly large Shootinge Star, which seemed to issue from Morrslieb itself. According to my calculations, the Star must have fallen to earth in the Uplands of Talabeclande, near the Head-waters of the River Narne.
