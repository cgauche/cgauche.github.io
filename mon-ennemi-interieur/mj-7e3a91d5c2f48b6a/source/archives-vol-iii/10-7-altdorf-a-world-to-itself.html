# 7. <a class="line-anchor" id="L1"></a>Altdorf - A World to Itself

<a class="line-anchor" id="L2"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L3"></a>*Pages PDF 83-84*

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L4"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L5"></a>Altdorf — a World • • to Itself
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L6"></a>**Dwarfs (Altdorfer)**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L7"></a>Altdorf is a cosmopolitan place, home to communities with distinct characters of class, origin, and even Species. When creating a Character from a large city such as Altdorf, the basic Reikland character from the **WFRP** core rulebook does serve as a template for the average townsperson, with no strong ties to any particular demographic. However, within Altdorf there are those whose background does have a more marked effect on their Skills and Talents. Unlike Middenheim, where a notion of a single archetypal citizen might apply, in Altdorf, different Characters may be associated with different districts, or even different streets. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L8"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L9"></a>**Skills:** Consume Alcohol, Cool, Endurance, Evaluate, Intimidate, Intuition, Language (Khazalid), Lore (Dwarfs), Lore (Geology), Lore (Metallurgy), Melee (Basic), Trade (any one) 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L10"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L11"></a>**Talents:** Craftsman (Any) _or_ Resolute, Magic Resistance, Night Vision, Read/Write _or_ Relentless, Sturdy 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L12"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L13"></a>**South Bankers**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L14"></a>Folk from the South Bank tend to be well educated, and used to mixing with high society. Even servants and tradesmen of Mauerblumchen and Zwillingsgrab will be used to the airs and graces of the nobility and priestly caste. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L15"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L16"></a>**Humans (Altdorfer — South Banker)**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L17"></a>**Skills:** Animal Care, Charm, Cool, Gossip, Language (Any), Leadership, Lore (Any), Melee (Basic), Melee (Fencing), Ranged (Blackpowder), Ranged (Bow), Ride (Horse) 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L18"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L19"></a>**Talents:** Doomed, Etiquette (Any), Noble Blood _or_ Pure Soul, Read/Write _or_ Supportive, Savvy _or_ Suave 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L20"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L21"></a>**Eastenders**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L22"></a>Altdorf ’s East End is a teeming rookery where criminality, disease, and insurrectionist fervour run riot. It is home to Altdorf ’s Dwarf community, whose honour, integrity, and conscientiousness juxtapose with the wiles of their Human neighbours. The Imperial School of Engineers is also here, so a minority of the inhabitants are educated and eccentric. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L23"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L24"></a>**Humans (Altdorfer — Eastender)**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L25"></a>**Skills:** Cool, Evaluate, Gossip, Haggle, Intimidate, Intuition, Language (Battle or Thieves’ Tongue), Lore (Reikland), Melee (Brawling), Ranged (Blackpowder), Sleight of Hand, Stealth (Urban) 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L26"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L27"></a>**Talents:** Argumentative _or_ Flee!, Criminal _or_ 1 Random Talent, Doomed, Nimble Fingered _or_ Read/Write, Savvy _or_ Suave 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L28"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a>**The City North**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L30"></a>Altdorf's City North is home to a number of distinct communities. The largest district in the city is there, the Hexxerbezrik, however it is not a bustling area of residences and marketplaces, but a strange and haunted swathe of the city, housing the eldritch Colleges of Magic and their many ritual sites, arcane devices, and experimental edifices. Many people aside from wizards do live there, but they are not untouched by the experience. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L31"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L32"></a>Those who are not so enamoured of it point out that it is a haven for smugglers, racketeers, corrupt officials, and worse. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L33"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L34"></a>**Humans (Altdorfer — Hexxerbezrik)**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L35"></a>**Skills:** Cool, Evaluate, Gossip, Intimidate, Intuition, Language (Classical), Lore (Reikland), Lore (Magic), Melee (Basic), Perception, Sleight of Hand, Stealth (Urban) 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L36"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L37"></a>**Talents:** Doomed, Beneath Notice _or_ Read/Write, Savvy _or_ Suave, Second Sight _or_ Sixth Sense, 1 Random Talent 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L38"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L39"></a>Further east is the Docklands area, where the hard labour fuelling Altdorf ’s thriving commercial life is done. Those who love Altdorf salute the Docklands for its boisterousness and diversity, as ships from all over the world arrive to load and unload cargos before their crews take time to relax in the nearby Street of a Hundred Taverns.
