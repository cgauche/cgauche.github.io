## <a class="line-anchor" id="L8"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L9"></a>**Hasso Schroeter**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L10"></a>Hasso was born to a poor but respectable family of Artisans in the Wynd district. He was always a talented singer and took to an old lute the family owned with a degree of skill. This was fortunate for Hasso, for he was a poor student in all other subjects. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L11"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L12"></a>Middenheim is a good city for a halfway talented Busker, and whilst Hasso’s skill on the lute was nothing special, combined with his sweet voice he was able to draw an appreciative audience. Every day he would leave his home in the Wynd and journey to Freiburg, hoping to earn some pennies from the art lovers who live there. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L13"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L14"></a>On a good day Hasso brings back a handful of pennies, but he yearns for something more. He has a dream to emulate the grand deeds of the heroes and noblemen he sings of.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L30"></a>Humans (Middenheimer)
<a class="line-anchor" id="L31"></a>Citizens of Middenheim have grown up within a big cosmopolitan city, with strong links to trade, entertainment, and criminal life. Skills and Talents reflect the likelihood that such Characters have either learned how to earn a living working, or are good at networking with at least one section of the city’s complex society. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L32"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L33"></a>**Skills:** Bribery, Charm, Cool, Entertain (Any), Evaluate, Gossip, Haggle, Leadership, Lore (Middenheim), Melee (Basic), Ranged (Bow), Trade (choose one) 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L34"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L35"></a>**Talents:** Doomed, Etiquette (choose group) or Strong Back, 3 Random Talents 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L36"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L37"></a>_That Busk Umpire felt the need to tell me I was no Rallane Lafarel, that it was time to clear out of the Great Park, and I should give someone else a go. I told him he wants the wax cleaned out of his ears, but with only eight pennies and a rotten turnip to show for an hour long set, I suppose the punters agree._
