<a class="line-anchor" id="L11"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L12"></a>White Lions are considered native to the wilds of Chrace, but are occasionally encountered in the forests and plains of neighbouring Elven kingdoms. They are renowned for their size and strength. Their powerful frames are almost as tall as an Elven steed, and they can break bones with a swipe of their massive paws. Whilst they are ferocious and known to prey on those Elves who enter their territories, White Lions are not regarded as evil creatures, but savage beasts to be feared, respected, and occasionally nurtured.

<a class="line-anchor" id="L21"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L22"></a>White Lions are also known for their fidelity. Adult White Lions fight fiercely to defend their cubs and guard their dens. It is with great pride that the bodyguards of the phoenix king take the White Lion as their namesake, and they are even known to raise White Lion cubs to be fierce but loyal war beasts. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L23"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L24"></a>White Lions are so highly regarded by the Elves that they have made it into myth. It is said that when Kurnous, the god of the hunt, met Rahagra, the father of the White Lions, they fought for three days. Upon realising that neither would prevail over the other, god and beast formed a bond of brotherhood. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L25"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L26"></a>**Great Eagle**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L27"></a>These creatures are the last of a noble species that live among the peaks of the Annulii Mountains. Like the High Elves, Great Eagles are haughty creatures who remember insults and do not tolerate fools. It takes centuries to earn the trust of these noble creatures, and, thus far, only the Elves have done so. The Eagles view other species with distrust, if not loathing. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L28"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a>From the vantage point of their high aeries, the eagles keep a close watch on the world, carefully observing the deeds of ground-dwelling creatures. They often warn their Elven neighbours of suspicious activity or the presence of creatures of Chaos, and the wisest mages are said to converse with the Great Eagles of the mountains as easily as if they were passing the time of day with their own siblings. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L30"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L31"></a>The Great Eagles have always been firm allies to the Elves. The histories tell that the two species have fought together since the Daemonic invasions that followed the collapse of the Polar Warp Gates, but legend carries the friendship deeper into the past. Indeed, in some tales, it was Talyn, the king of the Eagles, who bore Ereth Khial away to the underworld at Asuryan’s command.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L40"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L41"></a>**Kraken**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L42"></a>The seas around the Shifting Isles are treacherous. The enchanted islands that make up this barrier move from place to place to foil invasions by Dark Elves or the Norse. Terrible sea creatures, such as Kraken and megalodon sharks, are known to make their homes there, and some Elven mages surmise that they may even be attracted by the powerful magics that set the islands in motion, or that their great size and power is exacerbated by the arcane power. Whilst these creatures prove a threat to Elven shipping, pilots keep note of their hunting grounds, and merchant adventurers hunt down any that show too keen a taste for blood. For potential invaders, the presence of such monsters among the Shifting Isles merely adds to the peril of navigating their way through. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L43"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L44"></a>The Kraken is a monstrous squid-like creature with many tentacles that end in cruel suckers and vicious barbs. It grabs its prey with these appendages and propels them into its evilly beaked mouth. Stories are commonly spread among sailors of Kraken plucking the crew from a ship’s deck with their tentacles, feasting on their bodies, and then leaving the deserted vessel to drift.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L57"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L58"></a>**Flamespyre Phoenix**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L59"></a>Phoenixes are said to be the chosen messengers of Asuryan and are popular subjects of folk tales and artwork in the kingdom of Eataine. They are often depicted as rising from the waves, for many of Eataine’s folk believe it was only through the Creator God’s intervention that Ulthuan was rescued from the ocean’s chill embrace. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L60"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L61"></a>Phoenixes dwell amongst the Flamespyres, great pillars of alabaster that stand sentinel around the Shrine of Asuryan that stands on an island to the south of Ulthuan’s Inner Sea. These constantly burn with magical flame, for _Aqshy_ , the Wind of Fire, gusts about the rocks as it is drawn towards the Isle of the Dead. Over generations, the Phoenixes of the Flamespyres have become attuned to magic, harnessing it at whim. Their plumage mirrors their nature. It is rare for two birds to bear the same markings, but all share the angry reds and vibrant yellows of a hungry flame.

<a class="line-anchor" id="L82"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L83"></a>When a Flamespyre Phoenix is enraged, its plumage explodes into a magical flame, causing the creature to leave a trail of fire in its wake. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L84"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L85"></a>**Attuned to Magic:** All Phoenixes are invigorated when the Winds of Magic blow and weakened by their absence. When using the Swirling Winds table in **Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay** , page 238, refer to the following table to determine the effect on Phoenixes. When not using the table, similar degrees of excess or insufficient magic cause a similar effect. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L86"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L87"></a>**Vulnerable to Cold:** Endurance Tests to withstand Cold Exposure ( **WFRP** , page 81) suffer a –4 SL penalty. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L88"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L89"></a>**Wake of Fire:** Any creature that comes into physical contact with a Flamespyre Phoenix, including those who are struck by them in melee, gains an _Ablaze_ Condition. Furthermore, when the Phoenix makes a Move, they may also spend their Action to shed a rain of burning cinders. All creatures under the path of the Phoenix must make a **Challenging (+0) Dodge** test or receive an _Ablaze_ Condition, and flammable objects will be set on fire. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L90"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L91"></a>**Phoenix Reborn:** Should a Flamespyre Phoenix be slain, they explode in a shower of flaming cinders. If the Winds of Magic are at an ebb, the creature perishes, yet if the air is suffused with magic, the fiery fragments recoalesce, with a booming inrush of air and a flash of light, the Phoenix is reborn to fight anew. When a Flamespyre Phoenix dies, they may take a special extra Action in which they attempt to cast the following spell as if they were a spellcaster with _Arcane Magic (Lore of Fire)_ and _Language (Magick) 50._ The spell can only be cast using _Aqshy_ the Lore of Fire and will be affected if is suffused or absent in the area at the time of the Phoenix’s death.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L95"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L96"></a>**Frostheart Phoenix**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L97"></a>As a Flamespyre Phoenix ages, their magical fires grow paler and less fierce. Eventually, they become living conduits of cold whose plumage glimmers with icy blues, chill purples, and brilliant whites. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L98"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L99"></a>Their body cools and even begins to sap heat from its surroundings. Finally, the plumage that once blazed with fire grows heavy with frost and ice. Once this occurs, the Phoenix leaves the Flamespyres, for the chill that surrounds it causes agony in its brightly burning kin. Most such Frostheart Phoenixes dwell in lonely exile on crags about the Eataine coast until their bodies finally freeze solid, leaving curious frozen statues along the crags and cliff tops. Others return to the Shrine of Asuryan, to offer their final days in service as war steeds. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L100"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L101"></a>Whilst a Frostheart Phoenix may not possess the rigour and fire they commanded in their youth, they are a much harder mount. The ice that sheathes their form is as hard as glass, and their foes find their own strength and resolve eaten away by their chill presence. Knowing they can no longer resurrect themselves in magical fire, the Frostheart Phoenixes fight even harder in defence of their homeland, determined to end their lives striking a final blow against the enemies of Ulthuan.
