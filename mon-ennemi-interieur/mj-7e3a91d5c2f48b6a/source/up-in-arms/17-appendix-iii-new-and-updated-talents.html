## <a class="line-anchor" id="L8"></a>**Beat Blade**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L9"></a>**Max:** Weapon Skill Bonus 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L10"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L11"></a>**Tests:** Melee for a Beat Blade 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L12"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L13"></a>You are trained to make sharp controlled blows to your opponent’s weapon, creating an opening for an attack or simply impeding an incoming attack. For your Action, you can choose to Beat Blade before rolling. Perform a Melee Test; if successful, the opposing Advantage Pool loses –1 Advantage, and loses a further –1 if you score 6 SL. This Test is not Opposed. This Talent is of no use if your opponent has no weapon or has a larger Size than you. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L14"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L15"></a>**Crew Commander**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L16"></a>**Max:** Initiative bonus 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L17"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L18"></a>**Tests:** Ranged Skill tests when firing a weapon with the _Crewed_ Flaw 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L19"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L20"></a>A Character with this talent is practised at managing crews of siege weapons and artillery pieces. The Character may make a **Challenging (+0) Leadership** Test to help a crew manning a weapon with the _Crewed_ Quality within earshot. If the Character passes the Test, the crew members may then use the Character’s Ranged Skill when shooting the weapon. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L21"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L22"></a>**Distract**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L23"></a>**Max:** Agility Bonus **Bonus Tests:** Athletics to Distract 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L24"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L25"></a>You are trained in simple movements to distract or startle your opponent, drawing eyes from your true intent. You may use your Move to perform a Distraction. This is resolved by an **Opposed Athletics/Cool** Test against your target. If you win, your opponent can generate no Advantage for their Pool until the end of the next Round. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L26"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L27"></a>**Drilled**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L28"></a>**Max:** Weapon Skill Bonus **Bonus Tests:** Melee Tests when beside an ally with Drilled 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L30"></a>You have been trained to fight shoulder-to-shoulder with other soldiers. Characters with the Drilled Talent count as two combatants when determining Losing Advantage (page 137). 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L31"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L32"></a>**Flee!**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L33"></a>**Max:** Agility Bonus 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L34"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L35"></a>**Tests:** Athletics when fleeing or when the Quarry in a pursuit 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L36"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L37"></a>When your life is on the line, you are capable of impressive bursts of speed. Your Movement Attribute counts as 1 higher when Fleeing or when you are the Quarry in a pursuit. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L38"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L39"></a>**Gunner**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L40"></a>**Max:** Dexterity Bonus 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L41"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L42"></a>You can reload blackpowder weapons with practiced ease. You add SL equal to your level in _Gunner_ to any Extended Test to reload a blackpowder weapon. Reloading a blackpowder weapon during combat also counts as an Assess action. You gain an extra +1 Advantage when making that Assess Test. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L43"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L44"></a>**Rapid Reload**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L45"></a>**Max:** Dexterity Bonus 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L46"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L47"></a>You can reload ranged weapons with practiced ease. You add SL equal to your level in _Rapid Reload_ to any Extended Test to reload a ranged weapon. Reloading a ranged weapon during combat also counts as an Assess action. You gain an extra +1 Advantage when making that Assess Test.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L52"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L53"></a>**Relentless**
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L54"></a>**Max:** 1
<a class="line-anchor" id="L55"></a>When you have your mind set on a target, there is nothing anyone can do to stop you reaching them. The advantage cost of the Flee from Harm action is reduced to 1. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L56"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L57"></a>**Reversal**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L58"></a>In addition, you treat your size as being the same as that of your mount for the purposes of resisting Fear and Terror caused solely by the size of your opponent, such is your confidence when mounted. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L59"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L60"></a>_**Example:** Another person on a horse would not cause Fear, but a Daemon or an Undead creature would._ 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L61"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L62"></a>**Max:** Weapon Skill Bonus 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L63"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L64"></a>**Tests:** Melee when defending 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L65"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L66"></a>You are used to desperate combats, able to turn even the direst circumstances to your advantage. If you win an Opposed Melee Test, instead of gaining +1 Advantage, you may take 1 Advantage from the opposing Advantage Pool and add it to your own. If you do this, you do not cause any Damage, even if it is your Turn in the Round. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L67"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L68"></a>**Roughrider**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L69"></a>**Max:** Agility Bonus 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L70"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L71"></a>**Shieldsman**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L72"></a>**Max:** Strength Bonus 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L73"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L74"></a>**Tests:** Any Test to defend with a shield 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L75"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L76"></a>You are skilled at using your shield to manoeuvre others in combat so you can take advantage of a desperate situation. When using a Shield to defend, once per turn, you may spend 2 Advantage to either cause Damage when you are attacked as if it were your Action _or_ to push your opponent 2 yards directly back from yourself and no longer count as engaged. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L77"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L78"></a>**Tests:** Ride Tests when in combat 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L79"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L80"></a>You are at home in the saddle in even the most difficult of circumstances, and know how to get the best out of your mount during conflict. Assuming you have the Ride skill, you can direct your mount to take an Action, not just a Move, without a Ride Test. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L81"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L82"></a>**Strike to Injure**
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L83"></a>**Max:** 1
<a class="line-anchor" id="L84"></a>You are an expert in striking your enemies’ most vulnerable areas. When rolling on a Critical Damage table, make two rolls and choose which result you prefer.
