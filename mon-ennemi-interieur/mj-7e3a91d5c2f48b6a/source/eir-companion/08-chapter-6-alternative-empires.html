# <a class="line-anchor" id="L1"></a>CHAPTER 6 Alternative Empires

<a class="line-anchor" id="L2"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L3"></a>*Pages PDF 49-55*

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L4"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L5"></a>Chapter 6 Alternative Empires
<a class="line-anchor" id="L6"></a>_**In which we examine possible futures for the Empire, and how you, the GM, might take advantage of each.**_ 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L7"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L8"></a>**But What If…?**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L9"></a>What if the Characters prevent that final battle with Sheerargetru? Far from a thrilling conclusion, the ending could feel anticlimactic. In this chapter, we take a closer look at the alternative outcomes mentioned in **Empire in Ruins** (see page 179) — how you can integrate them into the story, what the near-future of the Empire might be, and present an adventure suggestion worthy of powerful characters. Each table’s campaign is different, however, so use these suggestions as inspirations for your own alternate endings. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L10"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L11"></a>**Yann Zuntermein the Spider in His Web**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L12"></a>A master spy, Zuntermein may be a Chaos cultist, but he is not stupid. After the events of **Power Behind The Throne** and **The Horned Rat** , his informants alert him to the Characters’ growing significance. Meeting them at Shining Rock and noting how they reacted to the attempted assassination of Katarina, he acknowledges them as resourceful figures and, therefore, as possible threats to his plan. If he is wily, they could be potentially useful tools. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L13"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L14"></a>**How Zuntermein Wins**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L15"></a>Zuntermein’s plot entails the Crown Prince, well-groomed by his Nine Eyes tutors, first becoming Prince Regent for the ailing Emperor and then ascending to the throne in his own right. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L16"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L17"></a>However, three dangers hover over his plans: Gideon, Wasmeier and the party. First, he suspects Gideon of having his own agenda and that recovering Ghal Maraz is crucial to it. Second, Wasmeier schemes to replace him atop the Altdorf Purple Hand. He could wreck everything. Lastly, the Characters are far too capable for Zuntermein’s comfort. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L18"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L19"></a>Zuntermein must prevent the recovery of the Hammer in order to succeed. While the attempt to steal it in Altdorf ( **Empire in Ruins** , page 179) represents a last, desperate effort, he does have a card to play earlier — Wasmeier. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L20"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L21"></a>Once it becomes clear that a mission to recover Ghal Maraz is underway, Zuntermein plants information through intermediaries that Wasmeier has been sighted in Altdorf and that he was behind the assassination attempt. Zuntermein himself never appears, though clever investigative work by the Characters should uncover a trail back to him. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L22"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L23"></a>Following Zuntermein’s clues, the Characters find the safehouse in which Wasmeier hides — as well as a faked message identifying Quintus Fassbinder as a Purple Hand agent. Written in the same cipher they encountered in Middenheim, it accuses him of conducting research for the cult, something others would be all too willing to believe. With Wasmeier eliminated and Fassbinder discredited, the quest for the Hammer never occurs, the summoning never takes place, and Yann Zuntermein wins. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L24"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L25"></a>**The Empire Under Zuntermein**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L26"></a>Zuntermein first must consolidate power. If the Characters have not figured out his scheme, then they can still be useful. Soon after the events of **Empire in Ruins** , the Emperor’s condition worsens as the Purple Hand’s ‘doctor’ continues poisoning him. Zuntermein, manipulating Liepmund Holzkrug, has the Reikland Council declare Crown Prince Wolfgang PrinceRegent to rule in Karl-Franz’s name as both Grand Prince and Emperor. Wolfgang, under Zuntermein’s influence, takes on the Characters as ‘trusted agents’. Soon, they uncover ‘evidence’ — planted by Zuntermein — implicating Holzkrug in a plot to seize the throne, aided by Else Barnhelm of the _Schattenkabinett_ and General Erik Hoffman of the _Palisades_ . ( **Altdorf** , page 209). With these rivals eliminated, the new Prince-Regent ‘reforms’ the Black Chamber, making Zuntermein its head and giving him a seat on the Reikland Council, where he becomes _de facto_ prime minister. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L27"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L28"></a>Soon, the Palace announces that the ailments of Luitpold, Prince of Altdorf, have grown worse. He is leaving to ‘take the cure’ at the Great Hospice, and his mother, the Lady MariaLuise, will join him to see to his care, while the Prince-Regent oversees the Principality of Altdorf. Thanks to a generous gift from Wolfgang to the hospice for their perpetual upkeep, Zuntermein is confident that neither will cause him problems. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L30"></a>Now secure, Zuntermein implements the Purple Hand’s grand design — ‘reforms’ that break down the pillars of the Empire, weakening it against Chaos. In Reikland, Wolfgang frees all towns from any feudal obligations, leading to outrage among the nobility and joy among the burghers. An Imperial decree suppresses the Order of the Silver Hammer as an enemy of ‘free thought’, eliminates licenses for wizards, and reaffirms the mutant decree. Sigmar’s cult fractures as the Arch-Lectors in Nuln and Talabheim defy orders to obey the new edicts. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L31"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L32"></a>While implemented in Reikland, across the Empire the new decrees meet strong resistance in the Prime Estates. Some grand provinces simply ignore them, while others declare them ‘null and void’ and punish anyone who tries to implement them. A meeting of the Electoral Council the next year to settle matters fails utterly, with Graf Boris declaring the north’s secession and Grand Countess Emmanuelle of Nuln proclaiming neutrality. A civil war that lasts years then ravages the Empire. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L33"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L34"></a>Agents of Nuln
<a class="line-anchor" id="L35"></a>While she has declared neutrality in the continuing strife, Emmanuelle Liebwitz has not been sitting idle as the Empire collapses around her. Aware more than most of what is really going on, she has laid plans for an uprising that would restore the Holswig-Schliesteins and eliminate Yann Zuntermein. However, to succeed  she needs a legitimate claimant, and the only one is Karl-Franz’s son, Prince Luitpold, currently at the Grand Hospice with his sister and their mother, who is also Emmanuelle’s dearest friend. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L36"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L37"></a>She thus has to rescue Luitpold and his family. In need of deniable agents and recalling the Characters, Emmanuelle contacts them through intermediaries with an offer: rescue Maria-Luise and her family from the Great Hospice and get them to Nuln in return for noble titles and estates. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L38"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L39"></a>The task is not easy. Zuntermein has replaced many on the staff at the Great Hospice with _Spionwerber_ operatives and stationed a ‘reliable’ unit of Reiksguard cavalry nearby as an ‘honour guard’. The Grand Countess also insists on minimal bloodshed — if her agents were to harm any of the Shallyan priestesses there, the political repercussions would be disastrous. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L40"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L41"></a>The mission is thus not just extraction, but also a chase, as the Characters evade Zuntermein’s minions, not to mention hostile local powers. Perhaps their route takes them through Marienburg to the sea, or across the mountain passes and through the Dwarfholds of the Grey Mountains, to Bretonnia, whence they make their way to Tilea and reach Nuln weeks later by way of the Alimento tunnel ( **Archives of the Empire** , page 17). 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L42"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L43"></a>**Wasmeier’s Revenge**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L44"></a>Once the head of the Middenheim Purple Hand, Karl-Heinz Wasmeier has fallen far, reduced to a fugitive seeking sanctuary from the cult in Altdorf. But he has plans, and the presence of the Characters gives him the chance to fulfil them… as well as gain revenge. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L45"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L46"></a>**How Wasmeier Wins and How the Characters Can Help**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L47"></a>Wasmeier wishes to eliminate Zuntermein and assume control of the Purple Hand, taking control of Zuntermein’s schemes and ‘doing them right’. But, he needs to defeat his rival without alerting Zuntermein and destroying the cult. While not fully aware of Gideon’s plans, Zuntermein’s opposition to the Characters’ mission to retrieve Ghal Maraz gives him an attractive opportunity to use one set of enemies to destroy another. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L48"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L49"></a>His plans commence after the Characters’ arrival in Altdorf. After the attempt on Katarina’s life, Wasmeier through pawns drops hints warning of treason within the Black Chamber and around Wolfgang. At the emergency Electoral conclave ( **Empire in Ruins** , Chapter 7), a cultist loyal to Wasmeier on the Palace staff slips a note in a Character’s food or their pocket alerting them to a meeting of ‘conspirators and cultists’ taking place that night near the university. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L50"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L51"></a>The note is genuine. That night is one of ritual significance for the Purple Hand, and Zuntermein is conducting the ceremony, one which Wasmeier misses due to ‘sudden illness’. If the Characters raid the meeting, Zuntermein is exposed, if not killed outright. Wasmeier then assumes command of the Purple Hand. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L52"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L53"></a>Regardless of how the Characters take out Zuntermein, there is still the problem of the summoning; its success would ruin Wasmeier’s plans. Though he does not know the full picture, Wasmeier knows that someone ordered the replacement of the Reiksguard at the Reliquary with cultists; he assumes this is a crucial part of whatever Zuntermein is planning. He has orders forged that once again change the guard, this time replacing the cultists at the last minute with real, loyal Reiksguard Knights. With this switch, the Hammer does not vanish through the trap door and the sacrifice of the Grand Theogonist and Crown Prince do not take place. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L54"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L55"></a>The summoning fails, and Wasmeier wins. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L56"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L57"></a>**Crumbling Empire**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L58"></a>Though he has won, Karl-Heinz Wasmeier does not have Yann Zuntermein’s skills and resources. He does not control the Black Chamber, nor even the _Spionwerber_ . He does not gain a seat on the Reikland Council, so cannot dictate the direction of the Empire’s wealthiest province. Though his plans are large, his means are limited, and his need for revenge against the Characters warps his judgement. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L59"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L60"></a>But Wasmeier does have some strings to pull and is still an experienced conspirator. If at least three of the Nine Eyes survived, then Wasmeier retains the cult’s influence over the Crown Prince’s education. If any were eliminated by the Characters, he finds replacement tutors with impeccable credentials, thus only briefly delaying matters. Regardless, under an assumed name he takes the place of the Law Tutor, giving him direct access to Wolfgang. The poisoning of the Emperor continues, which leads to the Crown Prince becoming Prince Regent. If the Characters uncover the doctor’s plot, Wasmeier’s plans are only inconvenienced; the damage done in the Emperor’s name has severely weakened his standing with the Electors. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L61"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L62"></a>Through the influence of the Nine Eyes, he persuades Wolfgang to ‘reform’ Reikland’s judiciary. An Imperial decree establishes a ‘Reikland High Court,’ with authority to appoint all judges and 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L63"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L64"></a>hear all cases in the Grand Province, as well as the authority to revoke any local laws ‘for the sake of uniform justice’. A further decree grants this court authority throughout the Empire. The Chief Judge of this new tribunal is, of course, Karl-Heinz Wasmeier, the Crown Prince’s trusted adviser. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L65"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L66"></a>The reaction is furious. The nobility rails against having their ‘ancient privileges’ usurped, freistadts resist losing rights they had just won, and the cults are outraged at being subject to secular law. Even the age-old friendship with the Dwarfs fractures, as the new High Court rules all Dwarfs within the bounds of the Empire are subject to Imperial law, not that of the Dwarf Empire, breaking longstanding treaties. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L67"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L68"></a>The breakup of the Empire is even worse than under Zuntermein. Graf Boris declares himself ‘Wolf Emperor’ as Middenheim and the north secede, while Grand Countess Emmanuelle has herself crowned Empress, forming a breakaway Empire with Stirland and Ostermark. The other Grand Provinces choose sides, with only distant Ostland remaining loyal to Altdorf, and even some internal provinces rebel against their Electors, subtly influenced by Wasmeier’s agents. The control of the Colleges of Magic breaks down, as some wizards escape the new regime to seek patronage and protection from other Electors. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L69"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L70"></a>Foreign enemies see their chance, too. The Duke of Parravon begins a sixth war, intending to annex Ubersreik and the Vorbergland. Marienburg demands extensive concessions to supply anyone with needed funds. Greenskins raid from the Black Mountains, and the new Southern Empire has its hands full with both an Orc invasion from Black Fire Pass and the civil war. The chaos lasts for decades. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L71"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L72"></a>Upheaval in Ubersreik
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L73"></a>**Lieberung Wins (Posthumously)**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L74"></a>Though long dead by this point, Kastor Lieberung’s plans could still bear fruit, leading to the bleakest of all futures. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L75"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L76"></a>**Kastor Victorious**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L77"></a>For Lieberung’s plan to succeed, all the others have to fail. While the Nine Eyes must survive to complete their work. Ghal Maraz, on the other hand, is irrelevant. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L78"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L79"></a>Though busy transforming the Empire, Wasmeier has not forgotten the Characters. In fact, he is obsessed with revenge. The perilous situation in Ubersreik gives him the opportunity to exact it. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L80"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L81"></a>The Characters likely have had to flee Altdorf as Wasmeier gained greater and greater control. Perhaps they went to join Graf Boris in Middenheim, or the Grand Countess in Nuln. Regardless, at their host’s court they find a surprising guest: Leopold von Jungfreud. He had been hiding in Averheim, but had to flee again because of the turmoil there. The remaining von Jungfreuds are either arrested or dead, making Leopold the legitimate heir to the Duchy of Black Rock, the ancestral seat now under Altdorf’s control. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L82"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L83"></a>News has filtered back from Ubersreik that anger at the new laws has already led to riots, and that the burghers are willing to welcome the von Jungfreuds back. They offer to rise against Altdorf, but only if Leopold comes to lead them. The Elector is interested in anything that will weaken the Prince Regent. Emmanuelle, in particular, would give Ubersreik her protection in return for the von Jungfreuds swearing loyalty to her. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L84"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L85"></a>It is a trap. While anti-Altdorf sentiment is genuine, cultist spies in Wasmeier’s service infiltrated the rebel groups and planted the idea of rebellion, knowing that the Characters would likely be the ones sent. The plan is to arrest them at a meeting with the ‘rebels’ where final arrangements were to be made. Unable to resist, Wasmeier himself will lead the raid. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L86"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L87"></a>If they escape, the Characters have the opportunity to do two things at once — free Ubersreik from Altdorf’s clutches and finally kill Karl-Heinz Wasmeier. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L88"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L89"></a>Ironically, the Characters are in the best position to make this happen, however unwittingly. While there are opportunities in **Empire in Ruins** for the Characters to expose and neutralise both Zuntermein and Wasmeier, the summoning poses more difficulties. Delivering Ghal Maraz sets the final steps of Gideon’s plan in motion. Even Sheerargetru’s defeat means Lieberung has lost, for his summoning costs the Crown Prince’s life. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L90"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L91"></a>If Kastor Liberung’s double has survived, one of the Nine Eyes realises that this is not their leader, but still someone who may be useful. Similar to the ‘Wasmeier wins’ scenario, this person has learned of the replacement of the Reiksguard, something not part of the plan. Suspecting a double-cross, the cultist pretends to be an innocent scholar who has only just learned the truth. They inform the Character of the substitutions and, having heard of their ‘great exploits’, begs them to do something. However the Characters deal with the problem, eliminating the fake honour guard and somehow preventing Yorri’s death means no summoning. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L92"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L93"></a>If Zuntermein and Wasmeier have also been dealt with, then the late Kastor Lieberung wins. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L94"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L95"></a>**Lieberung’s Fruit: the Chaos Emperor**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L96"></a>In the event of a Lieberung victory, the Turmoil, if it occurs at all, ends quickly. Wisely advised by his tutors and becoming an inspirational leader, Wolfgang quickly eclipses his ailing uncle as de facto ruler of the Empire, if not Emperor in his own right. Moving more slowly than either Zuntermein or Wasmeier would, he first appeases Graf Boris by finding those who planned the assassination of Katarina and executing them. He mediates between the cults of Sigmar and Ulric, brokering peace between them. He implements reforms to benefit burghers and peasants in Reikland, but only suggests them elsewhere. They become so popular, however, that the other Electors cannot resist. Finally, at a formal electoral conclave, he proposes changes to the structure of the Empire itself, giving Graf Boris and Grand Countess Emmanuelle what they wanted, and convincing the others to acquiesce. With the restoration of peace and prosperity, it seems a golden age has begun.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L127"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L128"></a>Mission to Ulthuan
<a class="line-anchor" id="L129"></a>Months after Wolfgang crushes the last rebellions, the Characters find themselves summoned to a small village near Middenheim. There they meet old acquaintances — Janna Eberhauer, now High Wizard of Middenland, and a badly scarred Thyrus Gormann, former Supreme Patriarch of the Colleges of Magic, now in hiding and hunted by the Emperor’s spies. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L130"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L131"></a>Having barely survived and escaped his battle with Wolfgang, Gormann has spent the intervening months re-establishing contact with those still willing to fight back. Realising they need far more powerful magic to defeat the Champion in Shadows, they came to a conclusion — seek aid from the Elves, as was done in the time of Magnus the Pious. Only then would a rebellion have a chance of succeeding. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L132"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L133"></a>But the Elves withdrew from the Empire at the rise of Wolfgang. So, Gormann and Eberhauer want the Characters to carry out this mission to Ulthuan. A boat, navigator, and crew await them in Marienburg, but they have to get into the city and then out to sea, while avoiding Wolfgang’s agents and the minions of Jaan van de Kuypers. Head of the most powerful of the Ten, the families that control Marienburg, van de Kuypers has profited immensely from his dealings with Wolfgang and will stop at nothing to protect his interests. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L134"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L135"></a>In Ulthuan, the Characters find their reception chilly at best, most at the court in Saphery having written the Old World off as lost. Though the Phoenix King refuses to provide an army, with deft diplomacy they can convince Teclis and Anurion the Green ( **Altdorf** , page 164) of the need to defeat Wolfgang and win their agreement to help. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L136"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L137"></a>Getting to the Empire is the problem. Wolfgang’s spies are seemingly everywhere, and neither wizard can use his magic, for fear of revealing their presence too soon. It is up to the Characters to figure out the route to Middenheim, where Gormann, Eberhauer, and the Graf await. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L138"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L139"></a>Perhaps through Nordland’s minor ports, perhaps across Norsca and Kislev to arrive from the east, the way home will be fraught with peril no matter what. If they succeed, however, the Graf raises the banner of rebellion and the battle for the Empire begins. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L140"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L141"></a>**The Characters Triumphant**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L142"></a>But what if, in the end, the Characters foil everyone’s plans? 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L143"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L144"></a>**Not Likely, but How Could This Happen?**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L145"></a>With all the dangers the Characters face, with all the foes, the odds are heavily against them winning total victory. It is possible, though everything has to come together exactly right. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L146"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L147"></a>They must stop the summoning. Defeating Sheerargetru is not enough, for it almost certainly means the Crown Prince or the Emperor — or both — died to bring forth the daemon. Even protecting them only means that other, albeit imperfect, vessels such as Zuntermein or the Nine Eyes would be used instead. One means would be to prevent the Hammer from falling into Gideon’s hands, perhaps by discovering the trap door to the wine cellar from the Reliquary. This would lead to the arrest, interrogation, and confession of Ermine Liebwitz ( **Empire in Ruins** , page 192). Thus warned, the authorities move the ceremony to the Grand Cathedral of Sigmar ( **Altdorf** , page 95), where it goes off without a hitch, the presence of the Hammer preventing a summoning. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L148"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L149"></a>They must also stymie Zuntermein. If he is killed following his attempts to direct his spies to kill the Characters, his threat is over. They could also expose him at any time after meeting him at Shining Rock. They may learn of his machinations through uncovering the plot to poison the Emperor, or discovery of the truth about the Nine Eyes could lead back to him. Confident and arrogant, Zuntermein has discounted any threat from Wasmeier. If the Characters capture Wasmeier, he could trade what he knows of Zuntermein in return for leniency. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L150"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L151"></a>Stopping Wasmeier is the easiest to accomplish. Given their experiences in Middenheim, the Characters may kill him on sight. It is also likely that his unhinged rage at the Characters leads him to attack them, again leading to his death. And Zuntermein himself may set him up to distract the Characters, should they be getting close to the truth about the _Spionwerber_ chief. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L152"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L153"></a>The Nine Eyes tutors are key to Kastor Lieberung’s plans. Killing or exposing them ends their influence over Wolfgang. Convincing Wolfgang of the truth could be hard, but, if they save Karl-Franz and present the evidence to him, he would order the arrest of the tutors and Wolfgang’s ‘re-education’ by the Cult of Sigmar. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L154"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L155"></a>If all these pieces fall into place, then the Characters win. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L156"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L157"></a>**An Empire Reborn… Sort of**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L158"></a>Although safe from the schemes of a daemon, a master spy, a sorcerer, and a dead man, the Empire remains badly damaged and faces a shaky future. At the top, trust has broken down almost completely in Karl-Franz as Emperor and Grand Prince. Revealing that he was poisoned and controlled by a Chaos cult does little to restore good relations, while the exposure of the Nine Eyes plot makes Wolfgang unacceptable to the other Electors as Crown Prince. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L159"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L160"></a>The Grand Provinces themselves are in upheaval. Independence movements roil Nordland; Ostland and Talabecland remain at war; Nuln edges closer to seizing Sudenland; and the grandees of Reikland fear they could be the ‘next Ubersreik’. Meanwhile, violence continues to break out between the cults of Ulric and Sigmar as rumours spread among Ulricans that the cult of Sigmar consorts with Chaos. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L161"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L162"></a>To prevent all-out civil war, Karl-Franz calls another Electoral Conference, and this one succeeds. In the greatest reorganisation since Magnus the Pious, Hochland, Nordland, and Ostermark become Electoral Provinces. To compensate their former rulers, Middenland once again joins Middenheim, with Graf Boris ruling both. Similarly, Talabheim becomes part of Talabecland upon the marriage of Elise Krieglitz-Untern to Helmut Feuerbach, and Nuln absorbs Sudenland to form the Grand Province of Wissenland. The former ruling families are either deposed — the Krieglitzes, Toppenheimers, Bildhofens, and Alptraums — or replaced upon their extinction, as with the Tassenincks. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L163"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L164"></a>Long Live the Von Wittgendorfs!
<a class="line-anchor" id="L165"></a>Though the Empire is at relative peace, problems remain to test the Characters. Having defeated all the myriad plots and saved Karl-Franz, one of the Characters — the Kastor Lieberung double, if he still lives — is made a baron and receives the estates and castle of the late Wittgenstein family, along with a purse of 20,000 GCs. It is up to them, however, to restore the land and make it profitable. Neighbouring nobles may resent these parvenus, even if they are heroes. While private war is frowned upon in the wake of the Turmoil, there are other ways to take what someone else has, especially if they begin to make the area desirable again. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L166"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L167"></a>Imperial politics aside, the long, dark rule of the Wittgenstein family left its mark on the Characters’ new holding. Many secrets may remain in the ruins of the old castle, and perhaps the Skaven have not lost interest in finding them. Many of the Wittgensteins were mutants themselves, and their province became known among the Tainted as a welcoming place. Do the new lords cleanse it with fire, or have their experiences softened their hearts to at least some of those afflicted? And what if jealous rivals pass rumours of their tolerance back to the Witch Hunters? 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L168"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L169"></a>To stabilise his rule, Karl-Franz marries Maria-Luise and names their daughter, Alia, as Heir, her education overseen by a committee, including the cults of Sigmar, Ulric, and Verena. The former Crown Prince is named Prince of Vorbergland, which title and estates he can assume once he completes his re-education. Karl-Franz confirms Ubersreik as a freistadt, but compensates the von Jungfreuds. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L170"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L171"></a>Though spared the worst, the Empire remains in a delicate state. Foreign enemies look to take advantage of its weakness, resentful losers scheme to get back what was once theirs, while ever in the shadows lurk the Purple Hand and other cults, plotting to turn the Empire into a realm of Chaos.
