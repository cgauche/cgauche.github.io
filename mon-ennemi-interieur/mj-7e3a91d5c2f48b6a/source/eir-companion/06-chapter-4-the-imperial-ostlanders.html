# <a class="line-anchor" id="L1"></a>CHAPTER 4 The Imperial Ostlanders

<a class="line-anchor" id="L2"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L3"></a>*Pages PDF 35-44*

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L4"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L5"></a>Chapter 4 the Imperial Ostlanders
<a class="line-anchor" id="L6"></a>The south, east and western borders of the Empire are guarded by towering mountain ranges, vast bulwarks against the many enemies who wish to conquer or despoil. The Emperor’s Own Imperial Ostlanders are a regiment under the direct command of the Emperor himself, charged with scouting these frontiers for threats from without. Their remit is to travel across and beyond the borders, looking for threats and, should an invading army cross the mountains, track their progress while relaying reconnaissance back to Altdorf. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L7"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L8"></a>The Count of Ostland was determined to prove his point. He instructed his Barons to scour the hilly lands of the eastern Middle Mountains for tough highlanders. The resulting patrol was despatched to the Worlds Edge Mountains on a mission to determine the threat from Grom’s armies, pick off any small straggling bands and track the horde’s progress and disposition. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L9"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L10"></a>Since the days of Wilhelm the Wise (2429–2434 IC), the Imperial Ostlanders have mustered tough soldiers from the eastern foothills of the Middle Mountains. If the Empire was in a stronger state of unification, the individual Electors and provincial leaders would share intelligence with the Emperor, but years of experience have taught the military commanders in the Reikland that they cannot trust parochial nobles with such an important responsibility. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L11"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L12"></a>Colonel Reikhardt Mathis Seivers is the current leader of the Imperial Ostlanders. Seivers is a man of humble origins, more at ease in single combat with a brutish Orc than negotiating etiquette in the Imperial Palace. As befits a scout regiment, the Ostlanders are a small force, but their professionalism and Sigmarite pride more than make up for their numbers. Decades of campaigning has hardened the regiment and ingrained an instinctive understanding of potential foes, but they have a specific knowledge of Greenskins, the most common threat from the mountains. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L13"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L14"></a>**History**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L15"></a>When Grom the Paunch threatened to rampage across the Empire with his Greenskin hordes, Prince Wilhelm of Altdorf, bemoaned the ignorance of his commanders. None seem to know the strength of the Goblin forces, or any sense of the route the monstrous horde would travel. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L16"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L17"></a>During this time, when Grom and his Greenskin hordes posed a major threat, Emperor Dieter IV was more interested in choosing new tapestries than attending the fractious Councils of War with the Electors. Frustration boiled over when Prince Wilhelm decried the military’s ignorance by describing the situation as, _‘like fighting a greased hog while wearing a blindfold and armed with a turnip’_ . The Count of Ostland, Gerson von Tasseninck, pledged that he could muster a troop of scouts experienced in tracking and hunting Greenskins. The other Electors were dismissive. They needed battalions and artillery, wizards and alliances with the Dwarf Holds, not scruffy savages from the Middle Mountains. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L18"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L19"></a>The patrol picked up Grom’s forces after the Battle of Iron Gate (2410 IC), where the Dwarfs were unable to halt the Greenskins. The Ostlanders shadowed the invading Orcs from Karak Kadrin into the Empire, watching as Grom attracted more tribes from Peak Pass and beyond. As the horde grew in number, they passed warnings forward to the towns of Stirland and Talabecland. Grom’s horde seemed unstoppable, but the Imperial Ostlanders’ actions saved lives. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L20"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L21"></a>Black Fire Pass had flooded and the Ostlanders knew it was impassable for the Chaos Dwarf war engines. They guessed correctly that Tamurkhan’s forces would travel through Winter’s Teeth Pass instead. When the Murder Grinz Night Goblin tribes ambushed the Chaos army in the Pass, the Imperial Ostlanders raced north to warn Countess Emmanuelle and Margrave Olger Hoch of Wissenland. The populace fled for their lives and Nuln readied the defences, eventually repelling the invasion. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L22"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L23"></a>When Grom’s marauders reached the Reikland, the Ostlanders briefed Prince Wilhelm on the warbands invading his lands. Under his command, they took to the field and legend has it that one of their number saved Prince Wilhelm from an Orc spear. In 2429 IC, once the threat of Grom had passed and Wilhelm became Emperor, he instituted the Imperial Ostlanders to anticipate future invasions on behalf of the whole Empire. The patrol was officially made part of the Wolfenburg 2nd Regiment of Foot, though this was more to do with keeping the logistics of training and equipping down to a minimum, the Imperial Ostlanders have always had their own Colonel and act independently from their parent regiment. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L24"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L25"></a>The Imperial Ostlanders are not infallible. They have been poor when anticipating threats from the Grey Mountains — failing to scout the Duc de Parravon’s forces in the Fifth Parravon War in 2474 IC. Currently, their preoccupation with Orcs in the Badlands has diverted them from a far more pressing menace — the gathering forces of Azhag the Slaughterer. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L26"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L27"></a>**Today**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L28"></a>The patrol is still raised from the Ostland highlands — a place of petty baronies subservient to the Grand Prince, where the people live austere lives in the shadow of the great peaks. There are a handful of non-Ostlanders in the regiment, but they are rare and no efforts are made to make them welcome. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L30"></a>The patrol proved their worth on many occasions since. In 2465 IC, they scouted ahead of Antoine von Carstein’s undead legions. Thanks to their swift action, Emperor Mattheus II forged an alliance of Elector Counts to defeat the Vampire at the Battle of Schrolnetz Plain. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L31"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L32"></a>The main barracks are in Wolfenburg, where recruits are inducted in a secretive ritual. They must pledge allegiance to the Emperor, dedication to Sigmar and prove to the command that they understand the forces and approach to warfare of the Empire’s main foes — the Greenskins, the Beastmen, the Warriors of Chaos. There is a small secondary barracks in Fielbach south of Altdorf, where some troops over-winter ready for spring campaigning in the Black or Grey Mountains. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L33"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L34"></a>More recently, Tamurkhan the Maggot Lord and his army of Chaos rampaged through the Border Princes towards the southern Empire. Colonel Sievers and his men observed the coalition of Marauder Tribes, Chaos Dwarfs and Ogres when they fought the mercenaries of the Eagle Confederacy. They picked off lone warbands and captured a handful of Kurgan, who revealed Tamurkhan’s ultimate destination as Nuln. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L35"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L36"></a>Officially the regiment takes orders from the Emperor, who determines which borders or potential threats they must reconnoitre. In practice, it is Reiksmarshal Kurt Helborg who determines their orders. In the field, the Imperial Ostlanders are afforded a degree of freedom to act on initiative — lines of command can’t often reach their remote locations, and should they discover something of interest, they are free to change their mission. The Imperial Ostlanders are expected to neutralise small threats unassisted and report back with news of larger concerns, such as an army or large warband menacing the mountain passes. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L37"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L38"></a>The Ostlanders operate primarily as a mobile infantry unit, using commando tactics against their adversaries. Through stealth and ambush, they can wipe out small numbers of opponents without attracting attention to themselves. On rare occasions they take to the battlefield alongside other regiments, the core unit armed with halberds with hunters and swordsmen fighting as supporting detachments. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L39"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L40"></a>**Reputation**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L41"></a>In Altdorf and the Reikland, the Imperial Ostlanders have a good reputation. The military see them as dependable, resourceful provincials who embody professionalism and loyalty to the House of the Third Wilhelm. The more superior nobility value provincial deference to Reikland rule. In the Reikland Colonel Sievers is a minor celebrity, where news of his exploits or discoveries in far-flung locations are the subject of many rumours. The Colonel finds this intensely embarrassing. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L42"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L43"></a>The Imperial Ostlanders are popular in their home province. Ostland has traditionally supported Imperial unity, so it is a matter of pride if a son or daughter takes the Emperor’s shilling. In 2506 IC, the regiment alerted the Emperor and Elector Count of Ostland to a force of greenskins massing in the Middle Mountains. Thanks to their warning, a combined force of troops from Ostland and Altdorf led by Reiksmarshal Kurt Hellborg ended the threat at the Battle of the Plain, south of Wolfenburg. The intervention of the Imperial Ostlanders elevated them in the eyes of their province. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L44"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L45"></a>Elector Count von Tasseninck is ambivalent. The regiment showcases his province’s allegiance to the Empire and Sigmar, but raising troops for the Emperor is rather like a tax imposed on his domain. He instructed Colonel Sievers that the Imperial Ostlanders should not ‘waste time’ patrolling the borders of states hostile to their own, as such Sievers has been concentrating on patrolling the Black Mountains, which keeps Averland happy and avoids any entanglements with Talabecland. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L46"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L47"></a>The other provinces look favourably on the Imperial Ostlanders when they bolster their own defences against invasion. But local nobles are unconvinced. The regiment are sometimes assumed to be a show of force by the Emperor, or spies seeking intelligence for Altdorf. Since the annexation of Ubersreik, this feeling is more common. The rulers of Nordland, Middenland and Talabecland don’t refuse to cooperate with the Imperial Ostlanders, but there is clear hostility when they cross their lands. In some frontier towns and villages, the Imperial Ostlanders have a reputation for throwing their weight around, confident that they have the Emperor’s backing. On occasion, locals who hold back useful information have been given rough treatment. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L48"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L49"></a>**Operations**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L50"></a>The Imperial Ostlanders patrol mainly in the Black Mountains and Worlds Edge Mountains, passing through Black Fire Pass, Winter’s Teeth Pass and Peak Pass to scout beyond the mountains. Less often, they’re deployed to the Grey Mountains, Forest of Shadows and Middle Mountains. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L51"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L52"></a>In the field, they look for signs of hostile warbands moving through the mountain. The regiment spends weeks on expeditions, surviving in the mountains with their wits and experience. They speak with travellers, locals and mercenaries to discover anything unusual or amiss. They have cultivated an understanding with the Dwarfs of the Black Mountains and Karaz Ankor, to exchange information of interest. The Dwarf thanes like to remind the Emperor of ancient allegiances 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L53"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L54"></a>**Recent Developments**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L55"></a>For the past year, the Imperial Ostlanders patrolled the southern foothills of the Black Mountains, alert to the possibility of incursions from the Border Princes. They learned from Dwarf patrols that Orcs are massing east of Barak Varr and took a perilous excursion into the northern Badlands where they discovered the nascent horde of Warboss Vorgaz Ironjaw. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L56"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L57"></a>Colonel Sievers knows that an Orc Waaagh! grows with the aggression and ambition of its leader. Ironjaw has already subjugated several tribes in the area, after he and his retinue upset Gorfang Rotgut who chased them from Broken Tooth lands. The Red Face and Bloody Spear Goblins, the Stink Fist Orcs and the Snake Blood Savages have all come under Ironjaw’s rule. Other tribes in the vicinity are becoming agitated and aggressive — a sure sign that a Waaagh! is brewing. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L58"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L59"></a>The Imperial Ostlanders believe that swift action in the Badlands or Border Princes could stop an invasion long before it happens. But the Empire’s rulers are not receptive. Grand Countess Ludmilla  Alptraum of Averland is preoccupied with political matters in her province. The Mad Count Marius Leitdorf was enthusiastic at first, but then became excited by designs for a new fleet of carriages and lost interest. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L60"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L61"></a>On arrival in Altdorf, Colonel Sievers rushed to the Palace for an audience with the Emperor. Karl-Franz was evasive and when the Colonel finally met him, he was distracted and distant. He didn’t seem to know the commander of the Imperial Ostlanders and dismissed his concerns about Orc invasion as, _‘just one of many matters I shall attend to in time’_ . Sievers felt that Karl-Franz’s accent wasn’t quite right. He had, in fact, met the Emperor’s double. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L62"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L63"></a>Infighting and politics have muted Seivers’ warnings, but the Purple Hand are also involved. Yann Zuntermein implied to various Imperial dignitaries that the Imperial Ostlanders are in the employ of the Dwarfs, who seek to divert the Empire’s armies to their own ends in restoring Karaz Ankor. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L64"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L65"></a>While Sievers stays in Altdorf and tries in vain to rouse the interest of the military’s commanders, the remainder of the Imperial Ostlanders continue to patrol Black Fire Pass and seek more information from the caravans that pass through. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L66"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L67"></a>**The Regiment**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L68"></a>In the field, the Imperial Ostlanders wear the black and white of their home province, with a navy blue trim. Their tabards are emblazoned with the griffon from the Emperor’s coat of arms. When scouting or skirmishing, they wear dark-grey surcoats to blend in with their surroundings. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L69"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L70"></a>**Colonel Reikhardt Mathis Seivers**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L71"></a>There has been a Sievers in the Imperial Ostlanders since their foundation. The family are commoners, but generations of dedicated service have ingrained a deep loyalty to the regiment. As a child, Reikhardt’s father recounted tales of Orc-hunting and drilled his son in combat. He rose through the ranks, proving his ability on campaign and earning the trust and respect of his comradesin-arms. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L72"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L73"></a>Now elevated to a position of command, Sievers finds himself dealing with the nobility and Empire’s military authorities far more than he feels comfortable. He is a man of action, a grizzled veteran who feels most alive hunting Greenskins in the mountains and surviving on his wits, rather than lobbying for support or charming the ruling classes. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L74"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L75"></a>Sievers is concerned about developments south of the Black Mountains and Karl-Franz’s strange behaviour. His preoccupations have perhaps blinded him to the brewing civil war. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L76"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L77"></a>Given the events of Empire in Ruins, the Colonel may have fulfilled the hammer quest and become a hero — or been killed in the attempt.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L91"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L92"></a>**Father Eduard Von Tunz, Regimental Priest**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L93"></a>The Imperial Ostlanders are dedicated Sigmarites. Father Eduard von Tunz has the solemn duty to lead them in their devotions. Third son of a minor Dunkelpfad noble family, he has a fine singing voice and dry sense of humour. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L94"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L95"></a>Father von Tunz is muscular in body and faith, as befits a fighting member of the Sigmarite priesthood. In battle he leads from the front, but in urban settings he is more reticent, ill-atease with town folk. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L96"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L97"></a>He has a secret history — before he became an initiate of Sigmar, Father von Tunz was a murderous brigand on the Ferlangen-Wolfenburg road. He experienced a spectacular vision of Sigmar and turned his life to one of faith. None of his comrades-in-arms know of his past and he prefers to keep it that way. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L98"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L99"></a>**Flag Sergeant Borys Zielin, Second-In-Command**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L100"></a>Zielin is Seivers’s right hand man, a tall Ostlander with cropped grey hair and a prominent jaw. He carries the regiment’s banner in the field and takes great pride in his service to the Emperor. Zielin is a man of few words, but surprisingly well-spoken, with only the slightest Ostlander accent. Aside from Father von Tunz, he is probably the most devout Sigmarite in the regiment. When the priest is not present, he leads the Ostlanders in prayer. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L101"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L102"></a>Zielen was hit in the leg by a Chaos Dwarf blunderbuss during the Tamurkhan campaign two years ago. He walks with a pronounced limp and a sense of purpose.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L132"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L133"></a>**Scout Sergeant Alicja Bohun, Leader of the Vanguard**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L134"></a>All Imperial Ostlanders are capable scouts, but the Vanguard are expert mountain rangers, keen-eyed masters of tracking. Bohun is their wiry leader, a woman with a cold-burning hatred of Greenskins matched by her ability to trace their passing and hunt them down with impressive accuracy and timing. She is also the regiment’s musician, carrying the same warhorn that called the original Imperial Ostlanders to order when fighting Grom’s horde. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L135"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L136"></a>Bohun is respected by the hunters under her command, but the Ostlanders whisper that her loathing for Greenskins can make her reckless.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L169"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L170"></a>**Renia Grünewald — Emissary**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L171"></a>The Imperial Ostlanders need to liaise with diverse and unusual sources when on campaign. Colonel Sievers recruited Renia to negotiate with locals for information, shelter, and to smooth over potential conflicts. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L172"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L173"></a>Renia was born to a Tilean father and Ostlander mother in Wolfenburg. Now a weathered veteran in her 50s, she is knowledgeable in the language and culture of the Dwarfs, Bretonnians, various Border Principalities and Ogres. She also has personal contacts in many mercenary companies that ply their trade on the frontiers of the Empire.

<a class="line-anchor" id="L190"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L191"></a>Sebastian encourages his soldiers in various money-making schemes. He’s good humoured and popular within the regiment.

He keeps his views to himself, but should the opportunity present itself, he will betray the regiment for coin or to advance Ostland’s fortunes.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L207"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L208"></a>**Soldiers**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L209"></a>The troops are a mix of men and women mustered from the towns and villages in the foothills of the eastern Middle Mountains. They are generally proud to join the regiment, which in many cases is the only way to escape a hardscrabble life of farming or hunting. They train to fight with sword, halberd or bow — the latter are preferred to crossbows as they are cheaper and easier to maintain when spending weeks far from civilisation. Each of them is able to fight independently from one another, but the infantry are drilled to assume loose formation or ranked units on the battlefield when necessary. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L210"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L211"></a>Most Imperial Ostlanders have the typical dark hair and long moustaches of their fellow countrymen. They are loyal, tenacious and pragmatic. Most of them have a deep, sincere faith in Sigmar and dedication to the Emperor and Empire — any cynicism or humour at the expense of the establishment is viewed with hostility. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L212"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L213"></a>Years in the mountains have given rise to many superstitions amongst the soldiers. They carry small tokens said to bring protection from Sigmar — griffon talismans, carved boar’s head pendants, and knife handles fashioned from orc bone. They have a peculiar dry sense of humour full of in-jokes about Ostland and the foolishness of Kislevites and Talabeclanders. Non-Ostlanders face gentle ridicule, but if they react with good humour of their own they will receive hearty laughter in return.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L247"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L248"></a>**Encounters with Imperial Ostlanders**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L249"></a>In **Empire in Ruins** , the party will meet the Imperial Ostlanders in Black Fire Pass, but if you choose to use them in other contexts, they can provide local colour, support the party, or even act as adversaries. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L250"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L251"></a>The party may meet the Imperial Ostlanders in any frontier town in the foothills of the Grey, Middle, Black or Worlds Edge Mountains. When they are on leave they drink hard but never become unduly raucous. The Ostlanders will always attend Throng at the local Temple of Sigmar and the party could encounter them wherever the local Sigmarites congregate. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L252"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L253"></a>If the party wants to gain information about a remote mountain region, asking around in the Reikland, Ostland or on the frontiers could lead to the Imperial Ostlanders. They can provide advice, maps and even guide the party if they come with Imperial sanction or propose to share useful intelligence back with the regiment. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L254"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L255"></a>They could be encountered in the wilderness or in a mountain pass. The Imperial Ostlanders’ command will hail the party if they don’t look like a threat, as they’re always keen to know what travellers have seen on their journeys. If the Character acts suspiciously or hostile towards the Empire, the Ostlanders will try to capture and interrogate them to ascertain whether they represent a threat. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L256"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L257"></a>Characters may even join the Imperial Ostlanders, if they show an aptitude or resourcefulness in the mountains. They will be inducted into the regiment in Wolfenburg or Fielbach and then expected to serve on campaign. However, if Sievers or the other commanders feel they can trust the Character, they may be sent on a mission independently with the rest of the party. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L258"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L259"></a>If the party has a patron from the Imperial establishment, they may have them seconded to accompany the regiment. The patron will have their own reasons — perhaps to explore regions where the Imperial Ostlanders are on campaign, to seek information on their behalf. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L260"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L261"></a>**Campaign Hook**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L262"></a>In the distant Badlands, the Orcs are stirring. The great warlord Vorgaz Ironjaw is marauding through the land, conquering Greenskin tribes and bringing them to his banner. The more his reputation grows, the more Greenkins join him and the greater he grows in reputation and might. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L263"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L264"></a>Colonel Sievers’s warnings to the Emperor were ignored during the Turmoil and when the Empire reaches a more stable state, few are willing to devote time and effort to a remote threat. If the party impresses the Colonel or the Altdorf military authorities during the **Enemy Within** , then they may be asked to verify whether Vorgaz Ironjaw is anything to worry about. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L265"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L266"></a>**Paranoia or Prescience?**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L267"></a>Colonel Sievers (or Sergeant Zielin) approaches the party to ask them to help in persuading the Reiksmarshal that Waaagh! Ironjaw is a genuine threat. If they decide to help, they will need to navigate the corridors of power, secure support from the Cult of Sigmar and Prime Estates. If they are very successful, they could lead an army south, but more likely they’ll be given resources for an expedition with the Imperial Ostlanders to the Badlands. Either way, there are forces in Altdorf who would rather the expedition gets lost — and they work to place their own people with the regiment to ensure this happens. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L268"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L269"></a>**The Road South**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L270"></a>On a journey south, the party reunites with the new Elector Count of Averland, Marius Leitdorf. Depending on their prior dealings and their powers of persuasion, they may be able to secure additional resources or troops for the expedition. The road will continue through Black Fire Pass (where they may encounter the Madcraw Clan again) down through the Border Princes. In these lawless lands they will need to negotiate passage with a series of eclectic local rulers before reaching Barak Varr, sea hold of the Dwarfs. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L271"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L272"></a>**A Measure of Malice**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L273"></a>At the edge of the Badlands, the Ostlanders need to work with the Dwarfs and various disreputable mercenary bands to scout the area and ascertain the danger that Vorgaz represents. They will find out quite how powerful the Warlord has become and discover his ambitions to invade the Empire. They may also discover the weaknesses in his coalition of tribes and the rivalries which could prove his undoing. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L274"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L275"></a>**We Must Do Something**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L276"></a>The Third Battle of Blackfire Pass is fated to happen in 2520 IC, where Count Marius Leitdorf is destined to lose his life and the Emperor stops Vorgaz Ironjaw’s invasion before it crosses into Averland. But the skeins of fate or the Characters’ actions may change the future. The party may remove the threat of Waaagh! Ironjaw altogether or their actions could be the reason that his horde invades the Empire in 2520 rather than in the next few years. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L277"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L278"></a>There are several options — they could assassinate Vorgaz Ironjaw, foster division amongst the Greenskins or pit another powerful Warlord against him. Skarsnik, Gorfang Rotgut or the Skaven could be surreptitiously enlisted to hamper or halt the growing Waaagh! 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L279"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L280"></a>If they don’t succeed, Waaagh! Ironjaw grows and rampages north, sweeping up more tribes as it goes. The Madcraw Clan join, finally fulfilling their prophecies. On the route,  the Yellow Eye Gobins, the Bloodaxe Alliance and the Blackvenom Tribe are subjugated along with many others. The Greenskin horde leaves devastation through the Border Princes before reaching the southern end of Black Fire Pass...

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L283"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L284"></a>**Adventure Hooks**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L285"></a>If you don’t want to run a full campaign or have a party who have not completed the **Enemy Within** , the Imperial Ostlanders may inspire other adventures where the Characters join them on their missions: 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L286"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L287"></a>**The Conqueror Worm**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L288"></a>Two years ago, the Imperial Ostlanders captured and interrogated several Kurgan followers of Tamurkhan. What they learned helped save countless lives in Wissenland, but it came at an unknown cost. Trooper Ewald Huthaler interrogated a Kurgan chieftain, who died laughing as he revealed Tamurkhan’s ambitions. As he expired, a tiny daemonic fly escaped his mouth and settled on the Imperial Ostlander’s neck, where it laid its eggs and expired. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L289"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L290"></a>Over the next year, the egg hatched into a maggot that burrowed into Huthaler’s brain. The old soldier is now little more than a puppet to the daemonic infestation. Slowly but surely, the creature is working to infect the regiment with the Wasting Shakes and spread the disease to any settlement they pass through. Can the party discover the source of the sickness before succumbing themselves? 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L291"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L292"></a>**A Diplomatic Incident**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L293"></a>The Bretonnian Ambassador is furious. Louis Rochelle has complained to the Imperial Palace that the Emperor’s regiment was found spreading insurrection amongst the peasants of the Dukedom of Montfort. In truth, the Imperial Ostlanders were scouting the foothills for evidence of a military build-up — under orders from Graf Holzkrug. Several of their number were captured by men-at-arms and pressured to explain their presence. Now they’re being used as political pawns between Altdorf and Couronne, with little hope of escape. The party is asked to help by a patron who wants peaceful relations between the Empire and Bretonnia — can they extract the Ostlanders and help to diffuse the situation in Montfort? 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L294"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L295"></a>**Machinery of Mass Destruction**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L296"></a>The Border Prince Lord Durant of Akendorf sends an emissary to Averheim to beg for help. The Goblins are wielding gunpowder artillery! Surely it won’t be long before they use it against the Empire? The Imperial Ostlanders are sent to investigate with the party. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L297"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L298"></a>When they arrive in the Border Princes, they discover that Lord Durant was exaggerating slightly, but not without cause. A small band of Broken Nose Goblins have acquired a Dwarf weapon that they call the ‘Leadbelcher’. It is an organ gun and while Bogrub and Gutbug haven’t quite figured out the technical details, they’ve managed to fire it on enough _‘humies_ ’ and ‘ _stunties’_ to know they’re onto something big. Now they’re starting to make big plans... 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L299"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L300"></a>**Cui Bono?**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L301"></a>Scouting the Black Mountains near the old province of Solland, the Imperial Ostlanders learn that a troop of Orc mercenaries have been raiding settlements in the foothills. Survivors’ descriptions match the notorious Ruglud’s Armoured Orcs — a vicious band who wear looted heavy armour and carry crossbows, which they can occasionally even use. Sergeant Bohun would appreciate the party’s help, because the Orc mercenaries seem to be targeting specific holdings belonging to Baroness Katarina von Heisenberg.  There are several parties who might want to bring the baroness down — the Baron Vicentus Preiss from neighbouring Scharmbeck, Solland traditionalists and Nulner merchants who have heard that gold has been discovered in the region. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L302"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L303"></a>Who would be desperate enough to hire Orcs as mercenaries? And what will they tell the Imperial Ostlanders when they discover it’s the Baroness herself? 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L304"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L305"></a>**Frontier Failure**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L306"></a>Three days ago a caravan headed into the Border Princes was stopped on the orders of the Colonel. Though not quite within his remit, the group are particularly poorly equipped, and the Sievers fears the worst if he allows them to continue on. The caravan consists of several foppish young nobles, the most notable of whom is Liepmund von Trollensatz, a fiery young man set on making a name for himself. Trailing behind are a handful of barely armed retainers and their families, with a few meagre supplies packed onto two carts and a carriage more suitable to the cobbled streets of Nuln than a hard mountain pass. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L307"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L308"></a>Sievers has thought of every reason he can to detain the group, and is quickly running out of excuses. Von Trollensatz claims to have connections to powerful people, and the Colonel is cautious of drawing more trouble on himself and the Ostlanders, and is desperately looking for a solution that doesn’t involve allowing two dozen people to wander off to their deaths. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L309"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L310"></a>Most who leave for the Border Princes are fleeing something or other. If the Characters could find out quite why this group is leaving the Empire they may be attempt to convince von Trollensatz to turn back. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L311"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L312"></a>Alternatively they may be able to convince some of Trollensatz’s retainers or the other lesser nobles to mutiny, perhaps by regaling them with a few tales of what terrors lurk in wild places of the world. If the Characters lend a blade to any incipient mutiny it is certain to succeed. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L313"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L314"></a>Finally, they may decide to accompany the group and see them safely on their way. This could be a way to start a campaign in the wilds, perhaps an attractive idea for some adventurers tired of politics in the Empire.
