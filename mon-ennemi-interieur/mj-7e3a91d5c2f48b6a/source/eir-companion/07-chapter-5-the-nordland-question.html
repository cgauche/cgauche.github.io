# <a class="line-anchor" id="L1"></a>CHAPTER 5 The Nordland Question

<a class="line-anchor" id="L2"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L3"></a>*Pages PDF 45-48*

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L4"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L5"></a>Chapter 5 the Nordland Question
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L6"></a>**The White Wolf Reborn**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L7"></a>For many centuries there has been discord between Middenheim and its vassal Nordland. The Turmoil has opened old wounds as deeply-held resentments resurface and new ambitions grow. Now the proud independent folk of Nordland are rebelling against the dominance of the City of the White Wolf and some are taking it to the extreme. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L8"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L9"></a>**A Troubled Alliance**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L10"></a>Before 767 IC Nordland was a backwater frontier, dominated by the mysterious woodland enclaves of the Eonir and sparsely populated by the descendants of the Was Jutone tribe, who were never truly part of Sigmar’s original alliance. It wasn’t until Wilhelm ‘Bullshanks’ of Middenland brought together the petty kingdoms in the northwestern Empire that Nordland united under one ruler. Wilhelm was second son of the Graf of Middenland and the new province was no more than an extension of his father’s domain. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L11"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L12"></a>Since then, the province has always been on the fringes of the Empire, it’s fortunes and influence ebbing and flowing like the tide. When Nordland escaped the yoke of Middenheim, it had elector counts who stood alongside the Empire’s other rulers. The title passed between noble lineages — with names such as Salzwedel, von Moltke, Ostrein and Gausser making their mark on history. In recent centuries, the Nikses have come to prominence, after Baron Adolphus Nikse dominated the province during the Age of Three Emperors, thanks largely to support from the Wolf Emperor. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L13"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L14"></a>Since then Nordland has been firmly under Middenheim’s thumb and there have always been those who craved independence. For the last few decades, Norse raids on the coast intensified and the coastal settlements beseeched Middenheim for aid. None was forthcoming and Nordlanders became increasingly restless. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L15"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L16"></a>Two events in 2502 IC brought matters to a head. In the north, Baron Werner Nikse married his daughter Anika-Elise to Graf Boris Todbringer — cementing the alliance and emphasising the subordination of Nordland. In the south, Karl-Franz sought to become Emperor and needed support, not just from those with an electoral vote. While Nikse backed Todbringer, Nordmarshall Theodoric Gausser offered Karl-Franz the support of northern nobility if he promised to address the Norse threat. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L17"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L18"></a>The new Emperor’s first act was to honour his commitment. A great host rode north, accompanied by Celestial wizards, the Reiksguard, and Kislevite allies. At the battle of Nordland Fjord they routed a Skaeling army. Gausser was sidelined by the new Emperor, but recognised the advantage in loosening ties with Middenheim to build closer alliances with the Sigmarite south. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L19"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L20"></a>Since then Gausser, Baron Ludolf Köhler of Dietershafen, and the cult of Manann have spoken of a new sovereignty for Nordland. They secured tacit support in Altdorf from the Imperial Navy and cult of Sigmar, who want more political power in the north. The disorder of 2512 IC and the Purple Hand stoked up violent separatists against Middenheim. The Nordmarshall and his allies have no connection to the pockets of open rebellion, but neither do they seek to restrain them. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L21"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L22"></a>**Recent Events**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L23"></a>High Duke Gausser plans a coup, directed by the tactical genius of his ambitious mother, Duchess Ingrid Gausser. The most powerful Nordland nobles and the cult of Sigmar have pledged support to Gausser’s uprising. Nordland’s State Army is ostensibly loyal to Baron Nikse, but several notable and respected local nobles are poised to declare for Gausser and are likely to bring a significant portion of the state military with him. Despite both sides petitioning Laurelorn, the Elf Queen remains silent and aloof from Human politicking. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L24"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L25"></a>Gausser does not realise that the separatists have been manipulated by the Purple Hand, largely by fallen Magister Karl-Heinz Wasmeier. The former Law Lord is taking revenge on Middenheim, using separatists as puppets to attack Baron Heinrich, assassinate Katarina, and commit other atrocities. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L26"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L27"></a>**Key Personalities**
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L28"></a>**Nordmarshall High Duke Theodoric Gausser**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a>The Gaussers are an old Nordland family, who trace their line back to Gansser the Patient, supposedly the first war chief in the Silver Hills to acknowledge Sigmar’s divinity. Gausser has commanded Nordland’s State Army many times over the past twenty years — appointed as Nordmarshall Baron Helmut Nikse in a misguided attempt to placate a rival dynasty. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L30"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L31"></a>After fighting alongside the Emperor and Reiksmarshall Helborg in 2502–2503, Gausser often mixed with important political figures in Altdorf, to the extent that many in the south still mistakenly believe he rules Nordland. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L32"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L33"></a>The Nordmarshall is impatient and abrasive, with a restless ambition to sit as an electors and extend Nordland’s territory. He has no strong religious affiliations and pays respect to Ulric, Sigmar, and Manann primarily for political gain. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L34"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L35"></a>THE NORDL AND QUESTION 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L36"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L37"></a>**Dowager Duchess Ingrid Gausser**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L38"></a>The elderly duchess resents the Nikses for usurping her family’s ‘rightful’ rulership of Nordland. She masterminded the campaign against Middenheim’s rule and surreptitiously directed her son Theodoric’s politicking. A shady contact, working for Wasmeier has given her compromising information about numerous electors. Now she bides her time, waiting to leverage these secrets to restore Nordland’s electoral vote. Most importantly, she has evidence that Anika-Elise Nikse was a Chaos cultist in the form of intercepted missives sent between the former wife of the Graf and her fellow Jaded Sceptre members. It is not undeniable, but it is more than enough to throw the Nikses, and by extension the Todbringers, into disrepute. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L39"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L40"></a>**Baron Werner Nikse**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L41"></a>Baron Werner Nikse sees himself as a proud Ulrican statesman, but his transparent subservience to the Middenheim court has reduced him to a sycophant. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L42"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L43"></a>The baron’s ancestral holdings are on the borders of the Laurelorn Forest. The family claims a Nikse signed the Treaty of the Leaf ( **Archives of the Empire I** , Chapter 1) with the Eonir in 630 IC agreeing that Humans had the right to settle in Nordland. Years of intermarriage between the Todbringers and Nikses have intertwined Werner’s allegiance to the City of the White Wolf, and many Nordland Ulricans pledge their allegiance to Baron Werner. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L44"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L45"></a>**Baron Ludolf Köhler**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L46"></a>The Köhlers rule Dietershafen and have traditionally provided great admirals for the Imperial Navy. Baron Ludolf speaks for the coastal Nordlanders who feel no affinity for Middenheim or its puppet Nikse. The Baron is a pragmatic man, and has stopped short of making an enemy of the City of the White Wolf, but he has used the family’s connections with the Imperial Navy to secure military contracts for Dietershafen’s shipyards. While none could criticise his savvy dealmaking, it has not gone unnoticed that the wealth of Imperial Coin flowing into the port has bolstered the case for Nordland’s economic independence from Middenheim. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L47"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L48"></a>**High Priest Erich Granholm**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L49"></a>Granholm is Nordland’s preeminent Ulrican priest who oversees the cult from the great Temple of Ulric in Salzenmund. He is ambitious for Nordland and unconvinced by the new Ar-Ulric, whom he feels is more politician than Ulrican warrior. Nikse assumes he has the High Priest on his side, but Granholm knows which way the wind is blowing and will side with whomever promises to restore Nordland’s honour. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L50"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L51"></a>**After the Turmoil**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L52"></a>After the events of **Empire In Ruins** , agents of Gausser discreetly tell Graf Holzkrug and other power brokers that Baron Nikse’s daughter was a Chaos cultist — using evidence from Wasmeier to prove it. A quiet scandal follows, ending when the compromised Graf Todbringer, Altdorf and Ostland exert pressure to depose Nikse as Grand Baron of Nordland and Prince of Salzenmund. The official reason is an error of legitimacy, which keeps the horrible truth concealed. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L53"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L54"></a>Simultaneously, the majority of the State Army declare allegiance to Gausser. They ride to Salzenmund with the wouldbe elector at their head. He takes the Nordland runefang _Crow Feeder_ and summons the Nordland nobles, the Burgomeister of Salzenmund and the cult heads to Castle Salzenmund. He makes a grand announcement:that he has persuaded a majority of electors to restore Nordland’s electoral status. Averland, Ostland, Stirland, Ostermark, Talabecland, Wissenland, and two Sigmarite arch-lectors supported this move. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L55"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L56"></a>Graf Todbringer is furious, but Ar-Ulric counsels him to be patient — it is not the will of Ulric that Nordland should be brought to heel at this time. Todbringer bides his time, but still harbours ambitions to bring Nordland back under his control 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L57"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L58"></a>Late in 2512 IC Gausser meets with Karl-Franz and Baron Köhler to establish a new Nordland Fleet of the Imperial Navy. The new elector count soon overreaches when he makes aggressive moves towards Hochland, sends Baron Nachtmann to dispute the border with Ostland and restates his claim as Duke of Marienburg. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L59"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L60"></a>The separatist movement in Nordland does not go away. The more violent and demanding members agitate for greater aggression towards neighbouring provinces — eager to see Nordland ‘restored’ to a mythical prominence within the Empire. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L61"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L62"></a>**Using the Nordland Situation**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L63"></a>Many of these events are a backdrop to **Empire In Ruins** , but you can make more of them in your game. A whole campaign could play out with the Characters supporting Gausser’s coup, travelling throughout Nordland seeking support for the Nordmarshall. Alternatively, Graf Boris could dispatch them to uncover the independence movement and quell the rebellion. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L64"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L65"></a>You can drop the following encounter into **Empire In Ruins** to show the Characters some nuances of the Nordland situation. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L66"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L67"></a>**Conspirators’ Congress**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L68"></a>On the road to Castle Reikguard from Shining Rock, the Characters pass through Mittelweg. The village inn, the _Cock and Magpie,_ has been chosen for a low-key meeting between Nordlander powers and a representative of the supportive south. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L69"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L70"></a>Upstairs in a private chamber, Baron Köhler (representing the coastal Nordland nobility and the Imperial Navy) meets with High Capitular Werner Stolz, Wave Lord Otto Sider of the Salzenmund Temple of Manann and Duchess Ullana Velten of the Imperial Council of State ( **Altdorf** , page 20). All are travelling without fanfare to avoid undue attention. If the Characters pass a **Challenging (+0) Lore (Heraldry)** Test they can identify them from devices on the horses in the stables and some attendants drinking in the inn. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L71"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L72"></a>If the Characters find a way to eavesdrop upstairs, they glean the substance of the conversation; Duchess Velten is the only one who offers no opinions of her own. 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L73"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L74"></a>The navy and coastal baronies want independence from Middenheim, as Todbringer has no interest in maritime matters or seaborne threats. 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L75"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L76"></a>The cult of Sigmar can support independence, but Stolz would prefer to avoid open conflict with Middenheim. 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L77"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L78"></a>If the navy or cult of Manann are to support independence, separatist violence must cease. 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L79"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L80"></a>None trust Gausser — he’s ambitious and has his own territorial obsessions with the Wasteland. But he’s been laying political groundwork for a decade and is the only choice to take over from Nikse. 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L81"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L82"></a>If Gausser takes action, they will support him, but disavow him if he fails. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L83"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L84"></a>When Werner Stolz comes downstairs, he recognises the Characters from their time in the Middenheim court. He greets them according to their prior behaviour and seeks to discover whether they know anything about the meeting, explaining as necessary depending on their perceived allegiance: 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L85"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L86"></a>**Pro-Ulrican:** Stolz tries to convince the Characters he’s working to broker peace between the Nordland separatists and Middenheim. They can see through this on a successful **Opposed Challenging (+0) Charm** Test vs. Stolz’s **Charm** of 65. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L87"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L88"></a>**Pro-Sigmarite:** He explains that certain parties in Nordland wish to secure peaceful independence to check Ulrican aggression in the north and reduce separatist attacks 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L89"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L90"></a>If the Characters make no trouble for the conspirators, Stolz whispers something to Baron Köhler. Impressed by their reputation, he suggests they make contact with him should they ever find themselves in Dietershafen. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L91"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L92"></a>**A Night for Fighting**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L93"></a>When the Characters are passing through the Reikerbahn ( **Altdorf** , page 133) or down the Street of Hundred Taverns ( **Altdorf** , page 181) in Altdorf, they walk into a ruckus between a handful of troops in Nordland colours and a motley collection of dockhands and ne’er do wells. A drunken punch aimed their way pulls them into the fight. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L94"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L95"></a>This is a street brawl and nobody is using weapons, but the Characters can step back, break it up, choose sides, or remain scrupulously impartial and hit out at everyone. Eventually some burly bouncers wade in to break up the fight and move on the combatants. If the Characters sided with anyone, they get a drunken cheer and are invited for a drink in the _Seven Stars_ inn. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L96"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L97"></a>**Nordlanders:** If the Characters drink with the soldiers, they learn they’re from the Nordland Seahawks, a regiment of State Troops often assigned to other states by Nordmarshall Gausser to fulfill various treaties. They have been on manoeuvres in the Reikwald, but are going back to Nordland tomorrow. Ulli, the most vocal Nordlander, slurs in a thick accent that there’s a fight coming. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L98"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L99"></a>‘ _See, they’ll need us back. Sort out Baron Niske… Niske… I mean Nikse. He’s not a proper Nordlander, spends too much time in Middenheim doesn’t he? Gausser’s going to put it right. He’s a bastard. But he’s our bastard, see? He’ll make these southerners take Nordland seriously_ .’ 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L100"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L101"></a>The Nordlanders are friendly and indiscreet. They share anything about the Nordland situation that a well-connected soldier might know. They leave the Characters wishing them well and promising to show them a good time if they ever make it to Salzenmund. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L102"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L103"></a>**Dockhand Dregs:** If the party side against the Nordlanders, their new friends are members of the Hooks ( **Altdorf** , page 31). They explain that the Nordlanders came from the Seahawks regiment and they’d started the fight when someone called them Norscans. The Hooks can explain a little about the Nordland Seahawks: they hear the soldiers are going back to Nordland to fight the Ulrican Baron Nikse on behalf of the Sigmarites and fishermen or whatever it is they have up there. There are no hard feelings — this was a good-natured brawl and the Hooks respect the Seahawks’ fighting reputation.
