# <a class="line-anchor" id="L1"></a>APPENDIX III Hypnotism

<a class="line-anchor" id="L2"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L3"></a>*Page PDF 149*

<a class="line-anchor" id="L4"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L5"></a>AIII 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L6"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L7"></a>Appendix III • Hypnotism
<a class="line-anchor" id="L8"></a>Hypnotism is an Advanced Skill used by some entertainers and mystics; some also believe that it can have medical applications. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L9"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L10"></a>**Note:** The GM is advised to use their discretion when a Player uses this skill. Ensure that the hypnotist’s subject is comfortable and open to being hypnotised. Being able to alter the beliefes of an NPC can shortcut many interesting plots in other adventures — be careful before you allow Players to use this skill. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L11"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L12"></a>**The Skill**
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L13"></a>**Hypnotism (Int)** _**Advanced**_
<a class="line-anchor" id="L14"></a>This Skill lets you place another Character into a trance. While entranced, the target is only aware of your voice: they will answer questions truthfully, and may remember things that have been wiped from memory by trauma or magic. In addition, you may place orders in the subject’s subconscious mind, which will be carried out until the effects of the hypnosis are lifted. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L15"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L16"></a>To hypnotise another character, the subject’s attention must be kept on the hypnotist for at least a minute. During this time, the hypnotist places the subject in a trance, possibly using a swinging bauble, and speaks in a soothing monotone. If the subject is cooperative, the Hypnotism Test is a simple one: otherwise, it is resisted by the subject’s Willpower. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L17"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L18"></a>A successful Hypnotism Test indicates that the subject has been placed in a trance. A willing subject remains entranced until the hypnotist decides to end the session. An unwilling subject may make a **Cool** Test each round, opposed by the hypnotist’s **Hypnotism** Skill, in order to wake from the trance, but gains one _Stunned_ Condition if success is only Marginal. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L19"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L20"></a>**Questions**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L21"></a>The hypnotist may ask questions of an entranced subject, which must be answered truthfully. For each question after the first, the hypnotist must make a **Hypnotism** Test. For every Test after the first, a –5 penalty is applied. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L22"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L23"></a>If the Test succeeds, the question may be asked, and must be answered. A Marginal Failure means that the subject does not answer, but remains in a trance. Any worse failure accidentally ends the session: the subject awakes, gaining one _Stunned_ Condition on an Impressive Failure, and two on an Astounding Failure. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L24"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L25"></a>**Post-Hypnotic Suggestions**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L26"></a>Implanting a post-hypnotic suggestion requires a successful opposed Test between the hypnotist’s Hypnotism Skill, and the subject’s Willpower. If the subject already understands the nature of the suggestion, and actively desires to have it implanted — for example, if the suggestion is to forget something horrific that  has been causing nightmares, or even insanity — the hypnotist gains a +30 bonus, but the Test must still be made: some resistance remains, even in a willing mind. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L27"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L28"></a>Once the suggestion is implanted, the subject will continue to act on it until the task is complete — if it is a finite task — or until the suggestion is removed under hypnosis, whichever comes first. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L29"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L30"></a>**Careers**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L31"></a>At the GM’s option, _Hypnotism_ may be added to the second tier Skill lists of the following Careers. 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L32"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L33"></a>**Physician** ( **WFRP** , page 57), especially if the Character has shown a previous interest in the workings of the mind. 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L34"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L35"></a>**Priest** ( **WFRP** , page 58), deities of healing (to help with mental trauma), and truth (to question witnesses and recover lost memories). 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L36"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L37"></a>**Scholar** ( **WFRP** , page 59), especially if the Character has shown a previous interest in the workings of the mind. 

- <a class="line-anchor" id="L38"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L39"></a>**Wizard** ( **WFRP** , page 60), Grey (to confuse the mind), and Light (to assist thought and clarity). Also cult wizards of Slaanesh and Tzeentch.
