## ## <a class="line-anchor" id="L4"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L5"></a>Appendix II Weapons
<a class="line-anchor" id="L6"></a>The following pages present some new weapons typical of Wood Elves, Dwarfs, and Halflings. While these weapons are typically found in the hands of their respective species, there is nothing preventing any properly trained Character from using the weapons below. Note that the Availability for give for these weapons reflects the ease with which they can be found in areas with a strong presence of their respective species. Additionally, some weapons are almost never offered for sale to outsiders and must be obtained in other ways.

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L78"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L79"></a>**Bearded Axe**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L80"></a>Favoured by many Dwarfs for use in war, the heads of these axes curve downward toward the haft. This creates a gap between the blade and the haft that is perfect for ripping weapons from unwary enemies. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L81"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L82"></a>**Blackbriar Javelin**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L83"></a>Fashioned from the wood of trees whose spirits have become twisted and malign, these living weapons slowly drip a lethal poison. They are said to bring ill fortune, and this is certainly the case for those who must face them in battle. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L84"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L85"></a>**Eonir War Blade**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L86"></a>The finely made blades of the Eonir are crafted to be somewhat lighter than a typical Empire weapon, but their perfect balance lends itself to swift, precise strikes. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L87"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L88"></a>**Eonir Spear**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L89"></a>The bane of mounted forces, these wickedly tipped spears are somewhat lighter than those used in the Empire. Their points are tempered and sharpened by methods known only to the Wood Elves, and they punch through armour with ease. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L90"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L91"></a>**Iron Skillet**
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L92"></a>**Cinderblast Bomb**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L93"></a>These lethal weapons are indispensable in the tunnel fighting that goes on deep beneath the Old World as the Dwarfs desperately stave off attacks by Skaven, Goblins, and worse. These bombs are typically tossed into the midst of packed enemy forces, exploding in a blast of flame and shrapnel that is terrifying to behold. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L94"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L95"></a>**Drakefire Pistol**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L96"></a>A cunning example of Dwarf artifice, a Drakefire Pistol blasts targets with incendiary shot, often setting them ablaze. The particulars of its design are a carefully guarded secret of the Dwarfs, but it is doubtful if any other species could replicate their  intricacies even if the design was understood. They are a favourite of Dwarf's who face down the Skaven deep beneath the earth, as their foul fur burns very satisfactorily indeed. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L97"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L98"></a>**Drakefire Shot**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L99"></a>The incendiary ammunition used in Drakefire pistols is both dangerous and rare, and truly only suited for use in the those weapons. However, it may be loaded in to any Blackpowder weapon, which will function exactly once before exploding in the users hands. Resolve this as though a Fumble was rolled on every shot. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L100"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L101"></a>**Dwarf Crossbow**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L102"></a>Though the difference between Dwarf and Imperial crossbows is less pronounced that that between their Blackpowder weapons, Dwarf Crossbows are nevertheless built to much tighter tolerances than any human smith could match. As a result, they are more precise weapons. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L103"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L104"></a>Despite their smaller stature, the cast iron pots, pans and skillets of the Halflings are fashioned to be heavier than one might think. Some may laugh, but more than one blade has been turned from a fatal blow by a Halfling pan. Such scratched and pitted skillets are always given pride of place in Halfling homes. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L105"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L106"></a>**Nan's Cleaver**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L107"></a>Halfling Cooks care for their kitchenware as carefully as the most diligent warrior cares for their blade. No wonder then that many of these weapons see use in times of war, and more than one Goblin has fallen to a cleaver that was later used to prepare a hearty post-battle stew. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L108"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L109"></a>**Precision Shot and Powder**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L110"></a>Expertly prepared powder and carefully smoothed and rounded bullets can be combined to allow for more reliable, precise shots with any suitable Blackpowder weapon. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L111"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L112"></a>**Slayers Axe**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L113"></a>Slayers take great pride in their weapons, sharpening them almost ritualistically at every opportunity. The most famous of these have gained reputations almost as formidable as those who bear them. They are devastating weapons, and Slayers who wield them seem able to do so tirelessly despite their size. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L114"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L115"></a>**Starfire Shafts**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L116"></a>Wood hewn from the Starfire tree is anathema to corruption, and arrows created from it are imbued with a fierce and blazing spirit. Those struck by arrows are often set ablaze, and their use massed use in warfare is a terrible thing to behold. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L117"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L118"></a>**Swiftshiver Shafts**
## <a class="line-anchor" id="L119"></a>**Dwarf Hammer**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L120"></a>Dwarfs are known for using the same hammers in the forge during peacetime and on the battlefield in times of war. These heavy tools are perfect for cracking even the thickest Orc skull. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L121"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L122"></a>**Dwarf Handgun & Pistol**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L123"></a>Dwarfs often despair at what passes for gun smithing among the humans, whose weapons are often as dangerous to the bearer as their target. Dwarf blackpowder weapons are finely made and carefully reinforced, making them less prone to misfire and imbuing their projectiles with additional punch. 

<a class="line-anchor" id="L124"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L125"></a>Cut from a wood so light and strong that they seem to leap from the bow of their own accord, it is a surprisingly easy thing for a skilled archer to knock and shoot more than one of these arrows at once. 

## <a class="line-anchor" id="L126"></a><a class="line-anchor" id="L127"></a>**Wildwood Sword**
<a class="line-anchor" id="L128"></a>A two-handed blade with a haft of living wood, these weapons seem to move with a will of their own, striking more quickly than such a sizable weapon would seem capable of.
