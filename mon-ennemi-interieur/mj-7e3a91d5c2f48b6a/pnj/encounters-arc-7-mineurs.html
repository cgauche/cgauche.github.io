# Encounters Arc 7 — PNJ mineurs

> Fiche groupée des **PNJ mineurs Arc 7** rencontrés en cours de campagne avec **interactions courtes** (pas de fiche individuelle dédiée pour préserver lisibilité). Triés par chapitre canon.

## Arc 7 ch.1 S56 — Nordland separatists

### Nielsa Klammer — Demagogue (canon ch.1 l.262-279)
- **Tough woman thick grey plaits, sealskin cap, toad-like face**. *« serious, hectoring tone of voice hardened years shouting at dockhands fur hunters. Touch fanaticism in her eye, but she's cynical and pragmatic enough »*.
- **Leader Neues Emskrank separatists**. Manipulée par Wasmeier *« mountain man »* disguise.
- **WS 39 BS 24 Int 34 WP 37 Fel 38** + Animosity (Middenheimers) Armour 2.
- Si cornered : surrender + public trial Middenheim *« could rally more support back in Nordland »*.

### Sepp Wurnhauser — Artillerist (canon ch.1 l.281-295)
- Imperial Second Fleet boatswain ex. Patriotic obsessionnel Sigmar+Manann+Nordland.
- **WS 35 BS 39 Dex 49 Ag 40** + Ranged (Blackpowder/Engineering) 49, Sail 49.
- **Operated Helstorm Rocket Battery** vs Sky Wolf dirigible.

### Hanne, Boris, Niklas, Mikhal (4 separatists)
- *« Assemblage of various discontents from Neues Emskrank who blame their misfortune on Middenheim rule »* (canon ch.1 l.299).
- **WS 36 BS 30** + Prejudice (Middenheimers) Armour 1.
- Hand Axes. Easily influenced. Quayside rhetoric Nielsa.

## Arc 7 ch.1 S56 — Dirigible crew

### Nabbi Nodrisson + Sindri Suttrisdottir — Dwarf Pilots Engineers (canon ch.1 l.131-144)

- **M3 WS 45 BS 49 Int 42** + Magic Resistance, Night Vision, Tinker.

### Wolfgang Munchburger — Loup Blanc loyal
- *« We swore to stand by Ar-Ulric »* (canon ch.3 l.142). N'attaque pas, reste camp.

## Arc 7 ch.12 — Dwarf Chain transport
- **Gararoth** : Three-Axe Brewery Grenzstadt. Particularly taciturn Dwarf. Couvre PJ false half-barrels wagon.
- **Khardrif Inglepeak** : Dwarf Boatman. Western Clock Tower Averheim. Barge **The Pegasus** moored next **The Hidden Valley** inn. Hides PJ below deck (Weirdroot smell).
- **Durkan Orkisson** : Lengenfeld blacksmith specialising agricultural tools. Old even for Dwarf. *« moves slowly with aid two engraved sticks »*. Business run by grandson same name.
- **Captain Fimbur Dimzadson** : **The Iron Wind** sailing ship Nuln→Kemperbad. Tall tales travel adventure.
- **Able Kazriksson + Gurrin Ablesniz** : Kemperbad workshop. Ablesniz nephew tué (throat slit) par Cult Broken Fingers night attack.

### Martin Schmidt — Cult Broken Fingers leader
- *« Cult Broken Fingers numbers only five members, including Schmidt. Schmidt often receives visions (Holy Visions Talent) and his latest vision contained the Characters. He does not know why, but has decided they must die »* (canon ch.12 l.219).
- **WS 45 BS 45 WP 41** + Animosity (Kastor and Friends) + Ranged (Blunderbuss) +8 + Fast Shot + Holy Visions.
- **Splinter Main Pourpre Kemperbad**.

### Old Dirk — Swindler (ch.10 l.413)
- Skinny sheepdog **Captain** + worn pitchfork. *« Old Dirk is a swindler looking to make an easy shilling or two of travellers. The dog and pitchfork are stolen »*.

### Griswald Voit — Ulrican priest (ch.10 l.418)
- *« retired having accomplished nothing more than troop along one muddy road »*. Tension Sigmarite priest local. *« Wigbert pulls them aside »* (grandson). Use **Beate Moser profile** canon ch.10 l.426 (Hardy +4W, Bless Ulric, Holy Visions).
