# Sergeant Major Markus Oldheim

> **Sergent Major Reikland**, **veteran soldier with 8 men** Reikland Halberds. **Handlebar moustache** + lifelong scarred (mutant blood splatter). **Rencontre PJ ch.12 via Beastman ambush** Diesdorf forest. Allié si PJ honest about Hammer. **Leader défense Wolfshügel** civilians+soldiers combiné. **Oath holy by Ghal Maraz** post-victoire — *« if they ever need his sword, it's theirs »*.
