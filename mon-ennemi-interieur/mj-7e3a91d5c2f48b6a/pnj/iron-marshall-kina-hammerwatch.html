Iron Marshall Kina Hammerwatch MJ

Iron Marshall Kina Hammerwatch

Iron Marshall Dwarf, ancien combattante. Admet faux Ghal Maraz forgé par smiths de son clan (canon ch.12 l.24). Active Grimshank's Chain Dwarf transport network. Donne Grimshank's coin aux PJ post-récupération Hammer. Sait Hammer drained + suggère Wolfshügel Sacred Flame recharge.

Identité publique

  • Titre : Iron Marshall Dwarf. Veteran combattante.
  • Apparence canon (ch.12 l.7) : « elderly Dwarf, her grey hair neatly groomed into plaits that reach down to her waist. A large scar runs from her forehead to her jaw. Escorted by two heavily-armoured veteran Dwarfs ».
  • Personnalité : Politely flattering PJ. Forthright candide.
  • Statut : [VIVANTE — ALLIÉE Dwarf Chain]
  • Tags : #Hammerwatch #KinaHammerwatch #IronMarshall #Dwarf #GrimshanksChain #BlackFirePass #FauxMarteau #Wolfshügel #Arc7 #Ch12

The temple is thought to be located somewhere between what is now Altdorf and Nuln. Pas de statbloc EiR direct — Veteran Dwarf elderly noble équivalent.

  • Carrière probable : Veteran Dwarf Warrior + Iron Marshall (chef militaire clan).
  • Skills attendus : Cool, Endurance, Intimidate, Lore (Dwarf History, Khazalid, Reikspiel), Melee (Two-Handed), Leadership, Lore (Geology).
  • Talents attendus : Doomed (non précisé), Master Tradesman, Read/Write, Strong-Minded, Sturdy, Magic Resistance, Night Vision.
  • Trappings : Hammer, Throwing axes, Armour, Grimshank's coin (rune-painted gold).

Conversation (ch.12 l.7-32)

  • Offre excellent ale + spiced goat.
  • « Those that carry Ghal Maraz are true heroes for the hammer can only be wielded by the worthy » (flattering — actually anyone without Chaos taint can lift).
  • Conveys points :
    • Hammer drained from millennia Chaos corruption (canon ch.12 l.18).
    • Dwarfs believe Sigmar visited ancient Temple Ulric where hammer blessed during final journey.
    • Master Albertus Nyman of Streissen = visited temple + knows location.
    • Freeing Ghal Maraz set many events in motion : omens + dreams hammer symbolism flood Empire. Many attracted to seek it out.
  • Feels culpable smiths clan forged imitation.
  • Hands rune-painted gold Dwarf coin : « This mark will help provide safe passage along your route. Travel where you are directed, show the coin, and tell whoever meets you your destination. They will do what can be done to help you ».

Grimshank's Chain (ch.12 l.38-46)

  • Network Dwarf activé par members en cas besoin. Rare.
  • Transmission important messages + goods over long distances. Support + protect messenger.
  • Members usually eldest + most respected of community.
  • Knowledge passed within families oral tradition.
  • Activation : Grimshank's coin = token marked with rune of Grimshank.
  • Painted runes indicate date issuance = use specific + limited.
