Albertus Nyman MJ

Master Albertus Nyman

Loremaster Streissen, ancien University Chancellor (poste official Countess Averland). Honorary Dean. Spécialiste obscure Sigmar / parchments hérétiques sectes. Identifie Wolfshügel = Mother's Mound où Sigmar fit bénir Ghal Maraz par Ulric. Réfugié dans cellier bookmaker brother Vorn post-Leitdorf coup Streissen. Partner : Captain Neuer (Streissen College Watch).

Identité publique

  • Titre : Master Albertus Nyman + (ex-)University Chancellor of Streissen (Honorary Dean University).
  • Personnalité canon (ch.12 l.141) : « tired and drawn, is willing to tell the Characters all he knows ». Sage paisible.
  • Spécialité canon (ch.12 l.143) : « always been fascinated by various accounts of the story of Sigmar. Much of what he tells them is familiar. Found fragments of parchment detailing obscure lore about Sigmar... fragments were from books deemed heretical by the Cult of Sigmar in the first millennium. Speculates they emphasised Sigmar's Ulrican faith to a degree that embarrassed the early leaders of his cult ».

Localisation post-Leitdorf coup

  • Hidden cellar of his brother's premises : Vorn Nyman = bookmaker. « They have hidden there since Vorn has chosen to wait out events at home » (canon ch.12 l.128).
  • 6 fugitives : Albertus + Captain Neuer + 4 autres (Gerdt + Gertie elderly servants + 2).
  • Captain Neuer : Captain Streissen College Watch — « stands in front of them with his sword drawn and demands that they leave ». Showing Ghal Maraz → stunned silence → fall to knees Sign of Hammer.

Localisation des notes Wolfshügel

To find all the information an extended Hard (-20) Research Test must be made to a total of 8 SL, with each test taking an hour ».

Mother's Mound / Wolfshügel canon (ch.12 l.146-160)

  • Hill appelé Mother's Mound. Bâti par worshippers Old Faith.
  • From which gods surveyed whole world (canon).
  • Others say sacred long before Sigmar — fire premier d'Ulric allumé là.
  • Sigmar y fit prostration devant Ulric + Hammer placed in Sacred Flame of Ulric and renewed = canon Sacred Flame Wolfshügel.
  • Located near Diesdorf, 1 day travel.
  • Ancien pathway depuis river Diesdorf : moss-covered ogham stones jalonnent (path disparu).
  • Tunnel entrée temple sealed with rocks (par les anciens prêtres pour protéger temple).
