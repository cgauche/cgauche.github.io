# Reiksmarshall Kurt Helborg

> **Reiksmarshall de l'Empire**, commandant Empire's field armies. **Absent du climax ch.13** (canon — *« away at Ubersreik »*). Retourne post-climax. Ancien ami Karl-Franz — guffaws au démasquage *« Hah, I knew well you were only malingering! »*. Personnage canon majeur WHFB.

## Identité publique
- **Titre** : **Reiksmarshall Kurt Helborg** — *« commander of the Empire's field armies »* (canon ch.13 l.338).
- **Personnalité canon WHFB classique** : Reiksmarshall légendaire, escrimeur d'élite (Runefang **Klausnertz** classique). À EiR : mature commander Reikland Cercle Intérieur.
- **Surnommé « Reiksmarshall Kurt von Hellboring »** par Marius Leitdorf dans son insulte horse Daisy Kurt von Hellboring II (canon ch.9 l.367 jab).
- **Statut** : [VIVANT — ABSENT climax]

## Source canon
- **EiR ch.13 l.338** : *« Marshal Helborg, commander of the Empire's field armies, is away dealing with the crisis at Ubersreik. Much of the Reiksguard has gone with him »*.
- **EiR ch.13 l.640** post-climax : *« Grand Marshall Kurt Helborg arrives, somewhat bedraggled from his rushed return from Ubersreik. He bursts into the room to much kerfuffle, clearly irritated at having to ask permission to enter. On seeing the Emperor restored his frown subsides. He guffaws and says, 'Hah, I knew well you were only malingering!', before grasping Karl-Franz by the arm and embracing him. He begins to speak in earnest about the issues in Ubersreik »*.
- **EiR ch.13 l.640+** : consultations Nacht about Jungfreuds — *« can either be placated, at great cost to the reputation of the throne, or they can be put down with a force of 5,000 troops and much bloodshed »*.
- **EiR ch.13 final** : *« I will see to this matter myself, the first of many I am sure. Saddle Deathclaw! I ride this very night for Neufaljung »* (Karl-Franz lui-même, accompagne Helborg).

## Position canon WHFB
- **Reiksmarshall** = head Reiksguard Knights + field armies commander.
- Combat record légendaire. Escrimeur master.
- **Veteran** ami personnel Karl-Franz d'enfance.
