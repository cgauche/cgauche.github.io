# Hans Knees

> **Spy d'Emmanuelle von Liebwitz** à Nuln docks. **Uniforme harbourmaster** comme couverture. Bureaucratic delay tactics — retient Carroburg PJ pendant qu'Emmanuelle apprend leur mission. **Pas combattant** — agent d'information.

## Identité publique
- **Couverture** : Officier harbourmaster Nuln. Uniforme harbourmaster + chapeau ill-fitting+dusty.
- **Vrai rôle** : **Spy Silver 3** for **Grand Countess Emmanuelle von Liebwitz**.
- **Apparence canon (ch.9 l.209)** : *« wears uniform of harbourmaster's office; however, a Character passing Easy (+40) Perception notices that apart from his ill-fitting and dusty hat, everything else he wears never fits him perfectly, is well-tailored, and could be afforded on a mere assistant harbourmaster's salary »*.
- **Personnalité** : Charm + polite + amenable. Openly delaying PJ to make clear *« where the power lies »* (canon ch.9 l.232).
- **Statut** : [VIVANT — AGENT EMMANUELLE]
- **Tags** : `#HansKnees` `#Spy` `#Emmanuelle` `#Nuln` `#Harbourmaster` `#Carroburg` `#Bureaucratic` `#Arc7` `#Ch9`

- **EiR ch.9 l.202-244** *This Petty Pace + Hans Knees*.

## Plan canon
1. **Carroburg arrives Nuln** : Knees présente petite formalités. *« We require a disembarkation certificate, in triplicate... boarding pass for each of you, with a signed affidavit from a notarised official... ship's manifest roster, fill in for every member of the crew. Don't forget the ship's cat's date of birth »*.
2. **Délays minimum 2 jours** : *« it takes a day to dismantle the mast and rigging in order to get The Carroburg to fit under the city's bridges to join the River Aver, then another day to get out of the river traffic press »*.
3. **Probes PJ** : carefully extract details mission. **Subtle mais happy let PJ realise** quicker spill = quicker continuer.
4. **Sven dockhand + Winona barmaid** (Green Waterwheel inn recommended) = autres spies. Marines bavards + drinkers = info source.
5. **Reports findings Emmanuelle**.
6. **Emmanuelle decides response** :
   - Si **vraie mission** découverte = surprise mais aide (KF resté ami). Sets spies for return.
   - Si Orcs/Border Princes = mostly irrelevant.

## Comportement avec PJ
- **Charm + amenable** : recommande **Green Waterwheel** (Winona barmaid spy aussi). Pas concerné qu'PJ guess motives. Subtle.
- **Easy (+40) Perception** révèle uniforme ill-fitting.
- **Average (+20) Perception/Intuition** révèle pas concerné PJ guess.
- **Si défi** : sticks to story.
