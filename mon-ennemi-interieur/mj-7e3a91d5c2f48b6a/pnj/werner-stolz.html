# Werner Stolz

[Werner Stolz — Fandom](https://warhammerfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Werner_Stolz)

> **High Capitular of Sigmar à Middenheim** — prêtre Sigmar le plus senior à Middenheim. Annonce arrivée Volkmar délégation Altdorf S57. Robe salie post-attentat (taudis Fauschlag). **Equivalent Sigmar de l'Ar-Ulric Emil Valgeir** localement.

- **Statut** : [VIVANT — ALLIÉ Sigmar Middenheim]

He suggests urgent talks to mend relations between the faiths of Sigmar and Ulric. To that end, he has appointed High Capitular Volkmar Hindenstern as an ambassador extraordinary. The High Capitular wishes to speak to you forthwith regarding proposals the Cult of Sigmar believe will ameliorate tensions in the Empire »*.
