# Thyrus Gormann

> **Supreme Patriarch of the Colleges of Magic**. Invité surprise par l'empereur au conclave S62. **Tempérament explosif littéralement** — peut s'enflammer (head/beard) quand fâché. Pense tous les Electors sont des idiots. **Pragmatique loyaliste**, ne suffer pas les fools. Allié potentiel climax ch.13.

## Identité publique
- **Titre** : **Supreme Patriarch of the Colleges of Magic** (canon ch.7 l.58+) — chef des 8 Colleges of Magic (Gold, Jade, Bright/Aqshy, Light, Heaven, Amber, Amethyst, Grey).
- **Domaine** : très probable **Bright Order (Aqshy)** — temperament feu littéral (flames flicker around hair+beard quand fâché, canon ch.7 l.255 + l.503).
- **Personnalité canon** : *« thinks that all these people are idiots and that he has far better things to do than get involved in silly political squabbles, but an Imperial summons must be obeyed »*. *« Favours unity and does not suffer fools (gladly or otherwise) »* (canon ch.7 l.62). **Brusque, direct, impatient avec mondanités**.
- **Statut** : [VIVANT — NEUTRE ALLIÉ POTENTIEL]
- **Tags** : `#Thyrus` `#ThyrusGormann` `#SupremePatriarch` `#CollegesOfMagic` `#BrightCollege` `#Aqshy` `#Altdorf` `#Arc7` `#TempérementFeu` `#Loyaliste` `#Modéré`

- **EiR ch.7 l.59-65** *Thyrus Gormann Supreme Patriarch of the Colleges of Magic* — *« Surprising exception to the rule that allows only Electors at the conference table »*.
- **EiR ch.7 l.110-115** S62 conclave — entrée fracassante : *« Get out of my way, you clods! I'm invited! »*.
- **EiR ch.7 l.250-256** réaction Ghal Maraz brisé : *« flames flicker around his hair and beard and from the tips of his fingers. Someone shouts 'Run! He's going to lose control!' »*.

- **EiR ch.13 climax l.338** : *« Of the heads of the Colleges of Magic, only Supreme Patriarch Thyrus Gormann and the patriarchs of the Gold and Jade Colleges are present. The rest are away with the armies »*.

## MJ-only
- **Bright Order Aqshy** — Spellcaster (Lore of Fire), patriarchs canon WFRP : Pyromancer skills + Fireball + Cauterise + Dart of Fire + Pillar of Fire + Wall of Fire + Crown of Fire + Burning Bright + Ulysz's Flameblast etc.
- **Bonus +20 wizard Charm** pour calmer ch.7 l.500.
- **Climax ch.13** : Thyrus + Gold patriarch + Jade patriarch vs Sheerargetru. **Patriarchs Jade College peuvent perish** canon ch.13 l.417 *« the patriarch of the Jade College perishing while exchanging sorcerous bolts with the Lord of Change provides cover for the Characters »*.
- **Suspicion canon S62** : *« speculation that the Emperor invoked the High Patriarch to keep the Electors focused on Gormann's infamous temper rather than to challenge the Emperor's suggestions »* (canon ch.7 l.117).
- **Pas cultiste** : loyaliste sincère Empire. Allié climax.
